SchemaDiagram displays two schemas (`public` and `billing`) of a shop database in an interactive [ErDiagram](/docs/components/er-diagram) inside a frameless window with a custom [TitleBar](/docs/components/title-bar). Its nine tables cover self-references, junction tables, multiple foreign keys between the same two tables, and cross-schema references.

**Demonstrates:** [ErDiagram](/docs/components/er-diagram), [Canvas](/docs/components/canvas), [Card](/docs/components/card), and [TitleBar](/docs/components/title-bar).

## Run it

From the cloned repository root (see [Build from source](/docs/get-started/from-source)):

```sh
mvn -q -pl examples -am compile exec:exec -Dexample=SchemaDiagram
```

## What to try

- Click a table to highlight its foreign keys and related tables while dimming unrelated ones. Press **]** and **[** to step through related tables, or arrow keys to move between tables.
- Drag a table (or hold **Shift** with arrow keys) to move and pin it. Click **Arrange** to re-lay out unpinned tables or **Arrange all** to unpin everything.
- Turn the scroll wheel to pan and **Ctrl+wheel** (or **+**, **-**, **0**) to zoom in, zoom out to skeleton and name-only detail levels, and fit the diagram.
- Type in the filter box to isolate matching tables, or switch between **All columns**, **Keys only**, and **Collapsed**.
