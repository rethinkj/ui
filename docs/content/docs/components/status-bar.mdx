`StatusBar.statusBar` is the bar along the bottom of a window, on the window's background as a title bar is: parts at the left that give way in a narrow window, and parts at the right that keep their size. Use it for the connection, the caret's position, the encoding. `Card.footer` is the same for one card ([Card](/docs/components/card)).

## Example

```java snippet="status-bar"
```

| Part | |
| --- | --- |
| `StatusBar.text(t, text)` | Muted text |
| `StatusBar.item(t, icon, text, onPress)` | Text with an optional icon; pressable with a handler |
| `StatusBar.status(t, state, text, onPress)` | A dot in the state's colour before the text: `CONNECTED`, `IDLE`, `ERROR`, or `BUSY` with a spinner |
| `StatusBar.height(t)` | Its height, to keep a toast clear of it |

Null parts are left out. A part with a handler is a button, one stop for Tab, and leaves the focus where it was.
