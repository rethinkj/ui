`StatusBar.statusBar` is the bar along the bottom of a window: a band in the cards' color with a hairline along its top. Parts at the left give way in a narrow window, parts at the right keep their size, and a message takes the room between them. Use it for the connection, the caret's position, the encoding, and what just happened. `Card.footer` is the same for one card ([Card](/docs/components/card)). When cards lie on the window above the bar, leave a gap below them, as between the cards.

Javadoc: [`StatusBar`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/StatusBar.html).

## Example

```java snippet="status-bar"
```

| Part | |
| --- | --- |
| `StatusBar.text(text)` | Muted text |
| `StatusBar.item(icon, text, onPress)` | Text with an optional icon; pressable with a handler |
| `StatusBar.item(tone, icon, text, onPress)` | The same, tinted as a [Badge](/docs/components/badge) of the tone (`Badge.Kind`), such as changes in `ACCENT` |
| `StatusBar.status(state, text, onPress)` | The mark of a state before the text: `OK` a dot, `IDLE` a ring, `BUSY` a spinner, `WARNING` and `ERROR` a sign, with the part tinted |
| `StatusBar.group(label, parts...)` | Parts that belong together, with no hairline between them |
| `StatusBar.menu(part, state, items)` | Makes a part open a menu: a chevron after its text; give the part `state::toggle` as its handler |
| `StatusBar.height()` | Its height, to keep a toast clear of it |

Each end is a list of parts and groups, with a hairline between each two. Null parts are left out, and so is a group of none. `statusBar(start, message, end)` puts a part after the start, in the room the ends leave; in a narrow window it is cut first, then the start. A part whose text is cut shows the whole text in a tooltip.

## Keyboard

A part with a handler is a button and one stop for Tab. A press on it leaves the focus where it was. A part with a menu opens it with Enter, Space or the arrows.
