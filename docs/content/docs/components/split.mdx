title: Split
description: Resizable two-pane container split horizontally or vertically with a draggable divider.
---

`Split.split` arranges two panes side by side (`Direction.ROW`) or stacked vertically (`Direction.COLUMN`) with a draggable divider between them. `Split.cards` does the same for two cards on the window background, placing the resize handle in the gap between them. Splits can be nested freely.

Javadoc: [`Split`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/Split.html).

Store a `Split.Position` in your app state and pass `Split.cards(direction, position, reset, onMove, first, minFirst, second, minSecond)` (or `Split.split(...)`):

| Parameter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `position` | Current divider position (`Position.first(px)`, `Position.second(px)`, or `Position.share(fraction)`), optionally collapsed via `.collapse(Split.Part.FIRST)` or `.collapse(Split.Part.SECOND)` |
| `reset` | Position restored on double-click or Enter when open, or a collapsed position to toggle collapsing that part while keeping the current size/share (or `null`) |
| `onMove` | Handler receiving the updated `Position`, including collapse and restore (`null` locks the divider) |
| `minFirst`, `minSecond` | Minimum pixel sizes for each pane |

Choosing the right `Position` controls resize behavior: `first(px)` keeps the left/top pane fixed (good for sidebars), `second(px)` keeps the right/bottom pane fixed, and `share(fraction)` keeps a proportional split.

## Collapsing

`position.collapse(Split.Part.FIRST)` or `position.collapse(Split.Part.SECOND)` collapses a pane while keeping its `kind` and `value`, so `position.restore()` returns to the last open position (`position.isCollapsed()` reports whether a pane is collapsed). A collapsed pane takes zero size and leaves the tree (no focus, pointer, or keys; what is kept of its elements, such as a scroll offset, starts anew on restore), and the other pane has no minimum then; in `Split.cards` the gap between cards goes too. The divider stays at the edge and can still be grabbed.

Pass a collapsed `reset` (such as `Split.Position.first(160).collapse(Split.Part.FIRST)`) to make double-clicking or pressing Enter on the divider toggle collapse instead of resetting; collapsing through that gesture keeps the current `kind` and `value` rather than `reset`'s. Dragging or pressing an arrow key away from a collapsed pane restores it, at least at its minimum.

## In a window

Leave a `theme.space().m()` gap between `Split.cards` and a [StatusBar](/docs/components/status-bar) below it, matching the spacing around and between the cards. Without this gap, the card edges and shadows touch the status bar's top border. When there is a title bar above, use `margin(0, m, m, m)`.

## Keyboard

The divider is one Tab stop (after the second pane). **Arrow keys** along the split direction move it by `theme.size().step()`, or restore a collapsed pane (at least at its minimum) when moving away from it. **Enter** restores a collapsed pane, or otherwise goes to `reset` (toggling collapse when `reset` is itself collapsed).
