`Slider.slider` is a rail with a knob, the part up to the knob in the accent. Use it for a value one adjusts by feel, such as a font size. For an exact number, use a [NumberField](/docs/components/number-field).

Javadoc: [`Slider`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/Slider.html).

`state.fontSize` is an `int` in the app's state.

`Slider.slider(value, min, max, step, onChange)`. Dragging moves the knob under the pointer by steps; a press on the rail puts it there. A null handler disables it.

The stops are `min` and every `step` after it. Where the range is not a whole number of steps, the last stop is the last one before `max`: End on 0 to 10 by 3 gives 9.

## Keyboard

The arrows step. Page Up and Page Down step by a tenth of the range, at least one step. Home and End go to the ends. Enter and Space do nothing.

## With a Prop

`slider(prop, min, max, step)` takes a [`Prop`](/docs/concepts/editing#components-that-take-a-prop), such as a field of an `Editing`, in place of the value and its handler.
