[CodeEditor](/docs/components/code-editor) shows icons, line numbers, version-control change bars, and toggles in its left gutter, draws diagnostic squiggles and inline messages in the code area, offers quick fixes at the cursor, and marks diagnostics, changes, and search matches along its vertical scrollbar.

Javadoc: [`CodeEditor.State`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/editor/CodeEditor.State.html), [`CodeEditor.Mark`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/editor/CodeEditor.Mark.html), [`CodeEditor.Diagnostic`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/editor/CodeEditor.Diagnostic.html), [`CodeEditor.Fix`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/editor/CodeEditor.Fix.html), [`CodeEditor.Marker`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/editor/CodeEditor.Marker.html), [`CodeEditor.Change`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/editor/CodeEditor.Change.html).

## Markers, markup layers and diagnostics

You can attach marks to `CodeEditor.State`:

- **Markers (`markers(list)`):** Clickable or static gutter icons with tooltips, at positions in the current string.
- **Markup layers (`markup(layer, text, list)`):** Named layers of `CodeEditor.Mark` items — `Diagnostic`, `Marker`, `Highlight` (a character background in `code().occurrence()`, such as usages of a symbol, or `Highlight(from, to, Highlight.Tone.NOTE)` for a whole-line `code().note()` tint, in place of the caret's line band, plus a thin gutter stripe at the code edge), `Change` (version control change bars), `InlayHint` (see [Inlay hints](/docs/components/code-editor/folding-and-hints#inlay-hints)), or `Block` (see [Blocks between lines](/docs/components/code-editor/folding-and-hints#blocks-between-lines)) — computed for `text`. Each provider uses its own layer name (such as `"analysis"`, `"vcs"`, `"references"`, `"hints"`, or `"app"`), and all layers show together. Marks move across edits elsewhere in the text without growing at their ends; a mark whose own text is edited is dropped, so is a `Marker` or `Block` at the start of deleted lines, and undoing back to `text` restores the originals. The editor finds the edits by comparing the old and new text, so a very large bulk edit, beyond its limits, can also drop marks between its changes. Clear a layer with `noMarkup(layer)`.
- **Diagnostics (`diagnostics(text, list)`):** Shorthand for `markup("diagnostics", text, list)` (and `noDiagnostics()` for `noMarkup("diagnostics")`). Draws a squiggly underline under errors (`ERROR`) or warnings (`WARNING`) and adds a gutter marker with the message tooltip. Diagnostics move with edits elsewhere, are dropped when their own characters are edited, and come back when undoing back to `text`.

When you pass the app, **F8** moves the cursor to the start of the next diagnostic and **Shift+F8** moves to the previous one, wrapping around at both ends. The diagnostic message appears above the cursor (styled like the marker's tooltip) while the editor has focus. It stays visible until the cursor leaves the diagnostic or you press **Escape**. **Escape** closes an open completion list or find bar first. If there are no diagnostics, these keys do nothing.

All positions (for `select(caret, anchor)`, diagnostics, and gutter markers) are UTF-16 offsets in the editor's normalized displayed text, where CRLF (`\r\n`) is normalized to LF (`\n`). To jump the cursor to a diagnostic, call `select(caret, anchor)` on `CodeEditor.State`.

## Marked blocks

`block(from, to)` highlights a range of lines on the gutter rail (for example, the SQL statement that **Ctrl+Enter** will run); call `noBlock()` to clear it. Call `blockOutline(true)` (off by default) to also draw a thin outline around the block's text in the code area:

## Gutter lanes

Each line's gutter cell has four lanes, in this order:

1. **Icons:** A reserved first slot for a diagnostic icon or the intention bulb on the cursor's line, followed by one slot per app `Marker` on that line (markup layers in order, then `markers(...)`). Several markers on one line show side by side. The lane is as wide as the most markers on any one line, so it keeps its width while you type. A rendered doc comment, whose lines share one cell, shows only the markers of its first line.
2. **Line numbers:** Right-aligned line numbers, primary text on the cursor's line and muted on all other lines.
3. **Change bar:** A lane that always keeps its width, so the code does not move when the first change appears, for `CodeEditor.Change(from, to, kind)` marks in a markup layer such as `"vcs"`. `ADDED` draws a full-height bar in `status().success()`, `MODIFIED` in `accent().edge()`, and `DELETED` (`from == to`) draws a short `status().danger()` bar at the top of the line where lines were removed before it, or at the bottom when `from` is the end of a line that is not empty, such as for lines removed at the end of the text. When a cell has both a bar and a deletion cap, or both top and bottom deletion caps, the caps draw independently over the bar in the same lane. `Change` marks also appear as ticks along the vertical scrollbar and move with edits like other markup ranges.
4. **Toggles:** The doc-comment render/edit toggle on standalone doc comments, or the fold chevron on foldable bracket ranges.

## Quick fixes

A `CodeEditor.Diagnostic` can carry a list of `CodeEditor.Fix(label, from, to, text)` edits whose offsets refer to the `text` passed to `diagnostics` or `markup`, in the same normalized positions as diagnostics. While the editor is editable and its text still matches that revision, moving the cursor onto a diagnostic replaces its gutter icon on the cursor's line with a clickable intention bulb, whose tooltip keeps the line's messages. Press **Alt+Enter** (or click the bulb) to open a menu of the fixes at the cursor; choosing one applies its replacement as a single undo step (**Ctrl+Z**). If the text changes to another revision, the fixes are not offered or applied.

## Inline diagnostics

Call `inlineDiagnostics(true)` on `CodeEditor.State` (off by default) to show the message of the most severe diagnostic on each line (`ERROR` over `WARNING`, then the first by position) muted after the line's end, in the severity's theme color and cut off at the field's edge; diagnostics with an empty message are skipped. A folded range's first line shows only diagnostics that start on that line, and lines replaced by a rendered doc comment show nothing.

## Scrollbar marks

While the code is taller than the view, its vertical scrollbar marks where the diagnostics and `Change` marks are, in their gutter colors, and, while the [find bar](/docs/components/find) is open, its matches. A press on a mark scrolls its line into the middle of the view, without moving the caret or the focus.

## In a tree dump

The editor shows diagnostic squiggles (`squiggle <from>-<to>`), `#rail`, `#gutter` containing `#line-<index>`, `#marker` (`role=button` with `label="Show quick fixes"` on the cursor's line when quick fixes are available), `#marker-<n>` for app markers, `#change` for VCS change bars (with `#change-top` and `#change-bottom` children when a cell has several change marks), `#note` for noted-line gutter stripes, and `#end-<line>` for each visible inline diagnostic message. When F8 shows a diagnostic message, it appears as an overlay on the editor with `role=tooltip` and the message as its `label`.
