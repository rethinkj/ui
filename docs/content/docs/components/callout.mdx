---
title: Callout
description: A note on a tint of its kind's colour with a bar and an icon in that colour, and optional actions.
---

`Callout.callout` is a note that stands out from the content around it: information, a warning, a danger or a success, with a title, a text and optional buttons, such as "Restart needed" with Restart. For a message that goes by itself, use a [Toast](/docs/components/toast); for a page with nothing to show, an [EmptyState](/docs/components/empty-state).

## Example

```java snippet="callout"
```

`Callout.callout(t, kind, title, text, actions...)`, with `Kind.INFO`, `WARNING`, `DANGER` or `SUCCESS`. `text` may be null. The info kind stays blue when the theme's accent changes, as links do. The colours of each kind come from the theme's `callout()` group (`Theme.Callouts`, `withCallout`).

The actions stand at the right beside the text while the text keeps at least `size().field()` (200) of width, and below the text otherwise, as in a narrow dialog. The callout needs no state and no width of its own for that.
