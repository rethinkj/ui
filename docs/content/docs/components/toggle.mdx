---
title: Toggle
description: A switch for a setting that applies at once, with its text beside it.
---

`Toggle.toggle` is a switch and its text in a row; a press anywhere on the row flips it. Use it for settings that take effect at once, such as Autocommit. For a choice that applies only when a form is saved, a [Checkbox](/docs/components/checkbox) reads better. For a switch in a settings list, use `Group.switchRow` ([Group](/docs/components/group)).

Javadoc: [`Toggle`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/Toggle.html).

## Example

```java snippet="toggle"
```

| Call | |
| --- | --- |
| `toggle(theme, on, text, onToggle)` | The switch and its text |
| `toggle(theme, on, onToggle)` | The switch alone; give it a `.label(...)` |

A null handler disables it. The row is as wide as the switch and its text, and so are its press area and focus ring.

## Keyboard

Tab reaches it; Space and Enter flip it. A press leaves the focus where it was.

## With a Prop

`toggle(theme, prop, text)` takes a [`Prop`](/docs/concepts/editing#components-that-take-a-prop), such as a field of an `Editing`, in place of the value and its handler.
