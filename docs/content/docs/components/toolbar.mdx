---
title: Toolbar
description: A row of buttons, toggles, split buttons and groups that moves what has no room into a More menu.
---

`Toolbar.toolbar` is a row of actions: icon buttons, buttons with text, toggles, split buttons, groups and a spacer. A group gathers tools that belong together on a tinted, rounded surface. The toolbar learns its width from the layout, and where the items do not fit, the last ones go into a menu behind More, one by one, also out of a group. It is one stop for Tab, with the arrows moving between items. For a card's title row with a few actions, `Card.toolbar` is enough ([Card](/docs/components/card)).

Javadoc: [`Toolbar`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/Toolbar.html), [`Toolbar.Item`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/Toolbar.Item.html).

`state.tools` is a `Toolbar.State` and `state.runMenu` a `Menu.State`, both in the app's state.

| Item | |
| --- | --- |
| `Toolbar.button(icon, label, onPress)` | An icon button with a tooltip |
| `Toolbar.text([variant,] icon, text, onPress)` | A button with text |
| `Toolbar.toggle(icon, label, on, onToggle)` | A toggle button; a checked item in the More menu |
| `Toolbar.split(variant, icon, text, onPress, menu, items)` | A split button; a submenu in the More menu |
| `Toolbar.group(items...)` | Buttons and toggles on a surface of their own; between lines in the More menu |
| `Toolbar.spacer()` | Room that pushes the rest to the right |

`.options(Button.options()...)` on a button, a button with text or a split button gives it a [button's options](/docs/components/button#options): its state, tone and shortcut; its icon stays the item's and its size the toolbar's. In the More menu, an item that is busy or off cannot be chosen, a split button that is off has no submenu, and the tone and shortcut show as the menu shows them.

After a change of width, the toolbar settles one frame later and new items fade in, so a script advances the clock before a snap. Narrow, Refresh has moved out of its group into More, with Export and Save:

## Keyboard

One stop for Tab; Left, Right, Home and End move the focus between items; Enter and Space press. A press leaves the focus where it was.
