`MenuBar.menuBar` is a row of titles, each opening its [Menu](/docs/components/menu) below it, as a desktop app's menu bar. It handles the pointer, Alt and F10, mnemonics such as Alt+F, and the key combinations of all items. Put it in the window's [TitleBar](/docs/components/title-bar), after the title: `TitleBar.titleBar(icon, title, List.of(menuBar), null, List.of())`. For a few actions, a [Toolbar](/docs/components/toolbar) or a dropdown is enough.

Javadoc: [`MenuBar`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/MenuBar.html), [`MenuBar.State`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/MenuBar.State.html).


- Each menu is a `Menu.submenu("&File", items)`. The `&` marks the mnemonic, the letter that opens the menu with Alt; `&&` is an ampersand.
- `state.bar` is a `MenuBar.State` in the app's state.
- `MenuBar.keys(menuBarState, menus)` on the root gives the bar Alt, F10 and the mnemonics, and runs the items' key combinations. `MenuBar.keysUp` handles Alt pressed and released alone. Combine them with other handlers with `Predicate.or`.

## What the user can do

- A press on a title opens its menu, and a press on the open title closes it. While a menu is open, a press on another title, or the pointer moving onto it, switches to that menu.
- Alt pressed and released alone, or F10, gives the bar the keys with the first title highlighted. Left and Right move, Home and End go to the first and the last title; Down, Up, Enter and Space open; a letter opens the menu of its mnemonic. Escape, Tab, Alt or F10 again, or a press outside leaves the bar.
- Alt with a mnemonic's letter opens that menu from anywhere.
- In a menu, Right on an item without a submenu goes to the next menu, and Left to the previous one. Escape steps back one level, then gives the focus back.
- Mnemonics are underlined while Alt is held or the bar has the keys.

The titles leave the focus where it is, so the menu gives it back to the editor. An item's key combination of Alt and a title's mnemonic throws, as two items with one combination do.

On Windows, Alt, F10 and Alt+Space reach the app; Alt+Space that no handler takes opens the window menu. Linux desktops take some combinations first, usually those with Super, Alt+Tab, Alt+F4 and Ctrl+Alt+F1 to F12.
