---
title: Popover
description: Any content below an anchor, such as a small form, that closes with Escape or a press outside.
---

`Popover.popover` hangs any content below its anchor while the app's `Popover.State` is open, as a menu without items: a filter's form below its button, details of a value. It is not modal: a press outside closes it and goes on. `Popover.form` is the common case, a heading, content and buttons. For a blocking question, use a [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog); for a hint on hover, a [Tooltip](/docs/components/tooltip).

## Example

```java snippet="popover"
```

| | |
| --- | --- |
| `Popover.popover(t, anchor, state, label, content)` | Content below the anchor |
| `Popover.popover(t, anchor, state, side, align, label, content)` | On another side, aligned `START`, `CENTER`, `END` or `STRETCH` |
| `Popover.form(t, anchor, state, title, content, buttons)` | A small form with a heading and buttons at the bottom right |
| `Popover.panel(t, state, label, content)` | The overlay alone, to attach yourself |
| `Popover.State` | `isOpen()`, `open()`, `close()`, `toggle()` |

The focus goes inside as it opens and back to the anchor as it closes; Tab walks it right after its anchor. It has no arrow to its anchor.
