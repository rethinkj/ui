---
title: Tooltip
description: A short text below an element after the pointer rests on it, or the keyboard focuses it.
---

`Tooltip.tooltip` shows a short text below its anchor once the pointer has rested on it for the theme's delay (half a second), and hides it when the pointer leaves or presses. It also shows when the keyboard gives the anchor the focus, until the focus leaves or a key goes down. Use it to name icon buttons and to show what is cut off. It holds no state. For content that the user interacts with, use a [Popover](/docs/components/popover).

`Tooltip.tooltip(theme, anchor, text)` returns the anchor. Where the tooltip would cover its anchor, it goes beside it. An idle window wakes for the delay and draws nothing while it waits. `Button.icon` already has a tooltip with its label.
