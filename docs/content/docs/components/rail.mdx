---
title: Rail
description: Vertical strip of icon buttons along a window edge for toggling tool windows.
---

`Rail.rail` renders a narrow vertical band of icon-only toggle buttons along the left or right edge of a window (`Side.LEFT` or `Side.RIGHT`), with a top group, a bottom group pinned to the bottom, and optional separators. Pressing a closed item calls `onSelect` with its index; pressing the open item closes it by passing `-1`. The rail knows nothing about `Split` or `Drawer` — pair it with `Split.Position.collapse` / `restore` to collapse and restore a side pane.

Javadoc: [`Rail`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/Rail.html), [`Rail.Item`](/api/com/rethinkj/ui/kit/Rail.Item.html).

Store a `Rail.State` in your app state. Item indices count `top` followed by `bottom` (`Rail.separator()` is not counted). Tooltips open toward the inside of the window (right of a `LEFT` rail, left of a `RIGHT` rail), showing any shortcut on keycaps and any dot's text as a description. When the rail runs out of height, it scrolls with the wheel and as the arrow keys move, without a scrollbar.

| Helper | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `Rail.item(icon, label)` | Icon toggle button with a tooltip |
| `.shortcut(modifiers, key)` | Shows a key combination as keycaps in the tooltip |
| `.dot(kind, text)` | Colored dot (`Badge.Kind`) at the button's top right, with `text` in the tooltip and tree dump |
| `Rail.separator()` | Horizontal hairline separating groups of items |

## Keyboard

One Tab stop. **Up/Down** arrows move between items across separators and groups (wrapping around), **Home/End** jump to first/last, and **Enter/Space** toggle the focused item. Clicking leaves keyboard focus where it was.
