---
title: Divider
description: A hairline across a column, with an optional label, or upright in a row.
---

`Divider.divider` is a hairline across a column; with a label it has a muted word in the middle, such as "or". `Divider.vertical` stands upright in a row, between groups of buttons. It draws in the theme's `border().divider()`. Use dividers sparingly: cards and space separate most things.
