- Added `DataGrid.Find` and `DataGrid.Options.find(App, DataGrid.Find)` so Ctrl+F in a focused `DataGrid` opens a floating find bar at the top right (with Match case, Regular expression, match counts up to `10000+`, Enter/F3 and Shift+Enter/Shift+F3 navigation, and Escape to close and return focus), tinting matches in visible cells and outlining, selecting, and scrolling to the current match without filtering rows; large grids search in time-budgeted slices on the app's thread, skipping unloaded rows.
