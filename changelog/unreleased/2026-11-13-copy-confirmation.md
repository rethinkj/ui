- `Button.copy(theme, clipboard, text, label, copied)` is the kit's copy button: a quiet icon button that copies the text and, with the app's `Button.Copied`, shows a check mark and a tooltip "Copied" for `motion().confirm()` seconds (a new token, 1.5 by default). `Markdown`'s code blocks confirm through `Markdown.State`, `Properties.properties` and `groups` take a `Button.Copied` in new overloads.
