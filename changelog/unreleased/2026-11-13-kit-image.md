- The kit's `Image.image(theme, image, alt)` shows an image with the theme's radius, or a placeholder with an icon and the description while the app has no image yet. `Markdown` shows images: `Markdown.options().images(address -> image)` gives the image for an address, which stands as a block at most as wide as the page, with its title as a tooltip and a placeholder until it arrives; an image that arrives later above the view leaves what is in view where it is.
