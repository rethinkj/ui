- `Tree.Options.drag(dragging, canDrag, canDrop, onDrop)` lets the user drag nodes and drop them before, after or into others. The tree shows a line or an outlined row where the node goes, scrolls near its edges, opens a closed node the pointer rests on, and reports a `Tree.Move` with the node, the target and `Tree.Place.BEFORE`, `AFTER` or `INTO`; the app moves the node. While the pointer of a drag rests over a drop target, the target now gets `Drop.Phase.OVER` again every `Drop.REPEAT` seconds, and `Drop.time()` gives the app's clock, so `Tabs` also scrolls along while the pointer waits at an end of the bar.
