- `CodeEditor.State.documentationInBackground(name, provider)`, `declarationInBackground(name, provider)` and `usagesInBackground(name, provider)` run quick documentation, declaration and usages lookups through `App.background`, cancelling superseded work, dropping results for outdated text, delaying Control+hover declaration lookups by `Theme.Motion.tooltipDelay`, and showing a `"Loading…"` row in the Alt+F7 usages menu until results arrive.
