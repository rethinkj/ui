- Added drags. `El.onDrag(handler)` gives an element the steps of a drag that begins on it, as `Drag` records of `START`, `MOVE`, `END` or `CANCEL` with the distance from where the pointer went down: once the pointer has moved more than `Drag.THRESHOLD` (4 logical pixels) with the button held, also outside the element and the window, until the release. A press that moved that far is not a press. All steps go to the handler the element had when the pointer went down; it gets `CANCEL` when the element leaves the tree or the pointer goes down again before a release. During a drag the dragged element is the one `App.hovered()` and `App.cursor()` report. A press on a descendant now stops at the nearest element with a press or a drag handler. A press in a text field stays the field's, also inside an element with a drag handler: moving selects text there, and until the release the field counts as hovered, as a dragged element does. `El.revealRow(index)` scrolls a list of rows to show that row whenever a rebuild changes it, for a selection that the keyboard moves.
