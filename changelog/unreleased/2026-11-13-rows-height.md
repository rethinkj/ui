- Fixed the height of a virtual list (`El.rows`) with millions of rows that grows or shrinks into the room it has: layout added the rows' full height in floats, which are not exact to the pixel beyond 2^24, so the data grid's list of a million rows came out a pixel short and left a strip at the bottom. Layout now sees at most 2^20 (1 048 576) pixels of rows; a list that nothing gives a height is at most that tall and scrolls through the rest. Scroll offsets and rows were already exact: the last of 100 million rows ends exactly at the content's end, and rows move by whole pixels while scrolling there.
