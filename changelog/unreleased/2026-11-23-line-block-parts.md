- Blocks between lines (`El#blocks`) in a field that does not wrap now scroll sideways with the text and are cut off at the content box, so they no longer cover the gutter. Elements in blocks, after a line's end (`El#lineEnds`) and in gutter cells show their `whenPressed` look while held, and overlays attached to them show in the frame that builds them. A `REPLACE` block lower than one row no longer lets its gutter cell overlap the next row, and `INSERT` blocks before line 0 also show while the field shows its placeholder.
