- `CommandPalette.palette` is a command palette at the top of the window: the commands of the app's menus (`CommandPalette.commands`) with their shortcuts, the last ones chosen first, other kinds of things after a prefix (`CommandPalette.Source`, such as `@` for a table), and second steps that preview a choice or ask for a text (`State.ask`). `CommandPalette.keys` opens it with Control+Shift+P and F1. `Find.goToLine(state, "42:7")` goes to a line of an editor's field. The Workbench, Showcase, connection dialog and settings examples use the palette and combo boxes.
