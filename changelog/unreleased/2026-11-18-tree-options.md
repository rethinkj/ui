- **Breaking:** `Tree.tree(model, options)` replaces the six-argument `Tree.tree`: `new Tree.Options<Node>()` takes `open(isOpen, onToggle)`, `select(selected, onSelect)` and `onActivate(handler)`, and `toggleOnDoublePress(predicate)`, with which a double press on a node, such as a table, only activates it. `Tree.Model.children` now returns `Tree.ready(list)`, `Tree.loading()`, `Tree.loading(text)` or `Tree.failed(message, action, onAction)` in place of null and the `loading` and `failure` methods. `Tree.Model.key` keys a node's row and mark by the app's key, so scripts find the node however the nodes above it open or close. The tree indents by `space().l()`, draws a guide below each open node's mark, marks the selected row with an accent bar, brightens a mark under the pointer, and shows a spinner in the loading row, which is `role=status`.
