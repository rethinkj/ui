- The kit's menus, select lists, tooltips, dialogs and toasts fade in as they open and out as they close, with the new `Theme.Motion.fade` spring; while one fades out it takes no input, and an idle window draws nothing once the fade is over. An overlay whose anchor fades in now shows from the anchor's first frame, as the focus already counted it, so that a submenu opened by a key in the frame its menu appeared in shows and takes the focus. Scripts that snap a menu advance the clock past the fade first.
