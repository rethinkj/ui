- Fixed the focus of an element that fades in (`El.motion`): `App.focus` and Tab now reach it from the frame that adds it, whatever its opacity on the way, so a field that a handler opened can be focused as soon as a frame has it, without waiting for it to show. An element the view makes invisible with `opacity(0)`, or one that is leaving, still takes no focus, and presses still reach an element only once it shows.
