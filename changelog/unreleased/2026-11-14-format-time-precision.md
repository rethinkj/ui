- `Format.time(precision)`, `Format.dateTime(precision)` and `Format.offsetDateTime(precision, Format.Offset)` write every time with its seconds and a fixed number of digits of their fraction (`09:00:00`, not `09:00`, so a column's times have one length), cutting further digits in the text only, and spell an offset as `Z`, `+00:00` or `+00`; they read what `TIME`, `DATE_TIME` and `OFFSET_DATE_TIME` read, and all three offsets.
