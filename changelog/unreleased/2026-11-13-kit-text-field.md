- Added `TextField.textField(theme, value, placeholder, onChange)` to the kit, a single-line text field in the theme's colors; a null handler disables it, and `.readOnly()` and `.maxLength(n)` chain onto it. `Theme.Accent` gained `selection`, the color behind selected text (the three-color constructor derives it from the accent), and `Theme.Sizes` gained `field`, the width of a text field (200 with the five-size constructor).
