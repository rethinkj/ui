- Tooltips have a lighter look (more room, the medium radius, a card's shadow, the small type) and wrap their text at the new `Theme.Sizes.tooltip`. `Tooltip.options()` adds a shortcut on keycaps, a description and a `Callout.Kind` with its icon and tint; `Tooltip.tooltip(anchor, () -> content)` shows any element, such as a Markdown document. Once a tooltip has shown, the next anchor the pointer reaches shows its own at once (`El.whenHoveredFor`). A tooltip takes no input: the new `El.inert()` lets presses through to what lies beneath and Tab pass its content by.
