- `App.notify(title, body, onClick, onError)` shows a system desktop notification on Windows 11 (WinRT toasts under the `Window.identity` AppUserModelID) and Linux (`org.freedesktop.Notifications` over D-Bus), raising the app's window and posting `onClick` to the app's thread when clicked while the app runs. Scripts record shown notifications in `<name>.notifications.txt` on `snap` and click them with `notification click "<title>"`; a test can set its own `Notifications` with `App.notifications`.
