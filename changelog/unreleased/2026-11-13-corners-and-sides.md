- `El.border(top, right, bottom, left, argb)` gives each side of a border its own width, and a side of 0 none, so a line along one edge of a box needs no element of its own; `El.radius(topLeft, topRight, bottomRight, bottomLeft)` gives each corner its own radius, which the fill, the border, the clip, shadows, the focus ring and presses follow; `El.focusRingGap(pixels)` draws the focus ring that far from the element, with what lies behind it showing between. The scene's instances are 96 bytes instead of 64, and its clip entries hold four radii.
