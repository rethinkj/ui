- **Breaking:** `Theme.Prose` holds every font `Markdown` and `Chat` draw, so `Theme.CURRENT.with(theme.withProse(...), ...)` swaps them for the whole app or only part of a view. Its constructor takes the new fonts `emphasis`, `strong`, `strongEmphasis`, `headingStrong`, `tableHeader`, `mono` (code, with its size) and `monoStrong`, whose defaults are the fonts `Markdown` used before. `Chat.composer` now types in `Theme.Prose.body()` like the user bubble shows the sent text, which is 14 instead of 13 pixels in the default themes.
