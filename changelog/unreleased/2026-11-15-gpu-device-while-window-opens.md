- A window's first frame comes sooner: `Window.open` now makes the GPU device and compiles the render pipeline on another thread while it opens the window, and the first `GpuPresenter` takes them over. An app can start that work earlier with `GpuPresenter.prepare()`, and `Instance.adopt()` hands a GPU instance and its objects to another thread. The page "Ship your app" shows how an AOT cache of the JDK starts a packaged app faster still.
