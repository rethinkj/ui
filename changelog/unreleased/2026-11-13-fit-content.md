- Added `Len.FIT_CONTENT`, CSS's `fit-content` as a width or height: the content's size, never stretched by `align-items: stretch`, while the parent's other alignments still apply. The kit's toggle and checkbox use it, so in a column their press area and focus ring end with their text instead of reaching across the column; `.alignSelf(Style.Align.START)` is no longer needed for that.
