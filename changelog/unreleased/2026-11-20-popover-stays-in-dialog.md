- An overlay beside its anchor in a dialog, such as a `Popover` or the one of `HuePicker.button` at the end of a form row, now keeps inside the dialog along its anchor: it aligns with the anchor's other end where that fits into the dialog, else it moves into the dialog as far as the window lets it, over the dialog's middle where it is wider than the dialog. Before, it kept only inside the window and could stick out far past the dialog's edge.
