- `DataGrid.Options.onActivate(row -> …)` reports the row a user opens with Enter or a double press on one of its cells or its number, so a grid that only reads can open a result or take a choice. Where Enter or a double press edits the cell (`onCommit`, `onEdit`), the grid edits instead. A double press on an editable column of a grid without `onCommit` no longer opens an editor that has nowhere to hand its value, as Enter and F2 already did not.
