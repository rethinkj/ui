- `CodeEditor.Diagnostic` can now carry `CodeEditor.Fix(label, from, to, text)` quick fixes: on the cursor's line where current-revision diagnostics at the cursor have fixes, the gutter shows a clickable intention bulb (`Symbol.LIGHTBULB`, `Texts.quickFixes`) and **Alt+Enter** (`CodeEditor.Command.QUICK_FIXES`) opens a menu of fixes at the cursor whose chosen edit applies as one undoable step; `Menu.State.openByKey` opens a menu with its first enabled item highlighted.
