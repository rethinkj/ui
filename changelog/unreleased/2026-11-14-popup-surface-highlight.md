- **Breaking:** Popups have a surface of their own and a neutral highlight. `Theme.Surfaces` has a `popup` (menus, a select's and a combo box's list, completions, the command palette, popovers, tooltips and toasts) and a `highlight` (the item under the pointer or the keys in a popup's list); its seven-argument constructor keeps popups raised. In `Theme.DARK` popups are a step lighter than controls, so that a menu stands apart from the card behind it, and the highlighted item is a neutral grey in both themes instead of the selection's blue tint, which now marks a selection only. `Menu.Item` has a `dangerous` component and `danger()`, which shows an item such as Delete in the theme's danger tone; a canonical `Menu.Item` constructor call needs the new argument.
