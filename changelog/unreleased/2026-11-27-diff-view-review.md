- `DiffView.State` now supports per-side markup layers (`markup(DiffView.Side, layer, text, marks)` and `noMarkup(side, layer)`, reserving `"diff"`) and a gutter `lineAction(symbol, label, onPress)` reporting `DiffView.Lines(side, first, last)`. Side by side, `CodeEditor.Block` marks pair with blocks on the opposite side or insert empty paired spacers (splitting hunk fillers around them) so both sides stay aligned as blocks grow and shrink, and lines carrying marks stay visible when unchanged regions collapse.
