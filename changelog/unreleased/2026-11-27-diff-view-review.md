- `DiffView.State` takes marks for either side with `markup(DiffView.Side, layer, text, marks)` and `noMarkup(side, layer)`, and a gutter action with `lineAction(symbol, label, onPress)` that reports `DiffView.Lines(side, first, last)`. Side by side, a `CodeEditor.Block` on one side gets empty space of the same height on the other, so review threads keep both sides aligned while they grow and while scrolling, and lines with marks never collapse. Fields coupled with `ScrollState.scrollWith` now map by pixels between anchors while either has paired blocks.
