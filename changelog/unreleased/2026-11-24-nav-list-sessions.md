- `NavList` serves a sidebar of sessions or documents: `Item.detail(text)` adds a quiet second line, `Item.trailing(element)` an element at the right such as a time and a status icon, and `Item.actions(buttons...)` buttons that show while the item is selected or under the pointer and do not select it. `Heading.count(text)` shows a count, and `Heading.open(open, onToggle)` makes a heading open and close its group; the app leaves a closed group's items out. The records `NavList.Item` and `NavList.Heading` have new components.
