- Text can be coloured by range: `El.highlight(key, highlighter)` takes a `Highlighter` that gives each line colours, other fonts of the same size, backgrounds and underlines (`Spans`), with a state carried from line to line for comments and strings. A multi-line field asks only about the lines it shows and keeps the answers per line until a line or the key changes. `El.decorate(decorator)` adds backgrounds and underlines of the lines in view to a multi-line field, such as the caret's line or a bracket pair, drawn behind the selection.
