- Fixed the height of a virtual list (`El.rows`) whose rows are together more than 2^24 pixels tall, about 760,000 rows of 22 pixels: layout gave it a height that was off by a pixel at a million rows and by tens of pixels at a hundred million, so that it stuck out of its parent. This also left the data grid's list of a million rows a pixel short, with a strip at the bottom. Layout now counts such a list as at most `El.LAYOUT_LIMIT` (2^20) pixels tall, and a list that nothing gives a height is at most that tall and scrolls through the rest; scrolling still reaches the last row, and rows move by whole pixels there.
