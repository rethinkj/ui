- Changed `DataGrid.dataGrid` to `dataGrid(theme, columns, rowCount, DataGrid.options()…)`, whose options turn on what the grid does while the state stays the app's. `.select(selection)` takes a `DataGrid.Selection` that the app keeps and the grid changes: a cursor, an anchor and ranges of whole rows (`Mode.ROWS`) or cells (`Mode.CELLS`), selected by a press, Shift and Control with a press, dragging (which scrolls beyond the grid's edges), the arrows, Page Up and Down, Home and End, Control+A and the row numbers of `.rowNumbers()`; a million selected rows are one range. `.copy(clipboard)` copies the selection with Control+C and Control+Insert as tab-separated text (`DataGrid.copyText`), up to `.copyLimit` cells, beyond which a handler gets the request; `.contextMenu(state, items)` opens Copy, Copy with headers and the app's items at a secondary press. `.sort(keys, onSort)` sorts by several columns, Shift with a press adding a key, with arrows from the icon font and their order in the header; `Column.sortable` says which columns sort. A double press on a header edge fits the column to its rows, dragging an edge beyond the grid's side scrolls it along, and `.frozen(n)` keeps the first columns and the row numbers at the left. Rows build only the cells of the columns in view, so hundreds of columns scroll as fast as a dozen. Text that does not fit ends in an ellipsis and shows whole in a tooltip. `.loading(loaded, onRows)` shows placeholders for rows the app has not fetched and tells it which rows the grid built; `.onEdit` asks the app to edit the cursor's cell on Enter, F2 or a double press. The tree dump says `gridcell`, `selected` and `current` for cells and rows. A grid that shows sort keys needs `rethinkj-ui-icons`; `Theme.Grid` has new tokens for the widest fitted column, the sort arrows and the cursor's outline.
