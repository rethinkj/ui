- The command palette opens 72 pixels below the window's top instead of right under the title bar, with a 32-pixel field, 28-pixel rows and 8 pixels of padding, from the new theme sizes `paletteTop`, `paletteField` and `paletteRow`. In a window too short for it, its list shows fewer rows and scrolls: an overlay at the window's edge (`atWindowEdge`) is now at most as large as the window less its gap. `TextField.withIcon` centres its icon in a field taller than a control.
