- Scrolling a virtual list (`El.rows`) no longer builds every row of the new range: a row it has already keeps its element, layout and motion, and its row function is not called again, unless the row shows or takes a look of the moment, such as the one under the pointer, or has a cycle, or the list's width or the part of it in view sideways changed. A frame of 120 pixels of wheel scrolling through a data grid of a million rows takes about a third of the time it did.
