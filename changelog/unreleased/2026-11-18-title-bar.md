- **Breaking:** `TitleBar.titleBar(icon, title, end...)` and `TitleBar.titleBar(icon, title, start, center, end)` replace the five `titleBar` and `withMenus` methods: the icon is any element, such as `AppIcon.appIcon(16, color, symbol)`, the menu bar goes in `start`, and actions go in `end`. The bar no longer takes `maximized`; it reads `WindowState.CURRENT`, which the app gives the view and builds again when the window is maximized, restored, activated or deactivated, so the title and the buttons' glyphs are muted in an inactive window. `TitleBar.search(placeholder, shortcut, onPress)` is a field for the middle, such as for a command palette, which makes the bar `Theme.Caption#tall` high. In a narrow window the middle gives way first, then the title shortens, while the buttons keep their width and a gap to drag stays. `HEIGHT` and `BUTTON` moved to `Theme.Caption` as `height`, `tall` and `button`, beside the buttons' `glyph` size. `Symbol.WEATHER_MOON` is new.
