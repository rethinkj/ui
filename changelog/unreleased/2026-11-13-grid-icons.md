- The data grid shows icons. A column made from a `DataGrid.DataType` (`new Column("id", 80, DataType.INTEGER, cell)`) takes its alignment, font and header icon from the kind of data; `.typeName("bigint")` puts the type after the title in muted text, `.icon(Symbol.KEY, Icon.Variant.WARNING)` sets the header's icon, and `.cellIcon(row -> CellIcon)` gives cells an icon before their text (`CellIcon.before`) or in its place (`CellIcon.instead`, which keeps the text as the cell's label, tooltip and copy), in an `Icon.Variant` colour and with a tooltip of its own. Where a column is narrow, the type gives way first, then the title; header tooltips show title and type, and fitting a column counts icons and type. `Symbol` has 20 more icons for the kinds of data and the parts of columns, among them `CALCULATOR`, `CALENDAR_CLOCK`, `CHECKBOX_CHECKED`, `FINGERPRINT`, `BOOK_NUMBER`, `TEXT_ASTERISK`, `PROHIBITED` and `RECORD`. `Icon.icon` now labels each icon with its symbol's name, so a `.tree.txt` shows `text label="chevron_right" "\u{F2AF}"` where it showed only the code point; tests that compare whole lines with icons need the label added.
