- **Breaking:** `Theme.Type` has a sixth style, `dialog` (16 px semibold), for a dialog's title; a theme built with `new Theme.Type(...)` passes it as the last argument. Cards sit lower than popups: the default themes' card shadow is a tight one of 1 and 2 px instead of a wide one of 6, so menus and popups stand out above them. `Card.header`'s title is in `type().heading()` (14 px semibold), like `Card.toolbar`'s, instead of 13 px medium. A padded card (`Card.card`) has 16 px of padding instead of 12, and a card's toolbar, filter bar and footer 16 at the sides, so their content lines up with a padded card's.
