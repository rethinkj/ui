- **Breaking:** `TabGroups` now manages a `TabGroups.Frame<T>` binary tree of split `Group<T>` panes per window: `Opener.open(Frame<T>, Window.Options)`, `main()` returning `Frame<T>`, `onWindowClose(Consumer<Frame<T>>)`, `tabs(Frame<T>, ...)`, `frames()`, `Frame.window()` / `groups()` / `active()`, `Group.frame()`, `dockAll(Frame)` / `closeAll(Frame)`, opt-in `splits(boolean)` (off by default) with **Split right** (`Ctrl+\`) and **Split down** context menu items, and `split(Group, int, Group, Side)`. Added `Tabs.Options.active(boolean)` and theme tokens `Theme.Accent.edgeMuted` and `Theme.Sizes.minTabGroup`.
