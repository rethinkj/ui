- `Completion.complete(theme, app, field, caret, state, suggest)` in the kit shows completions at the caret of a text field, such as a `CodeEditor`: a list of the app's entries for the word before the caret (`Completion.Entry` with text, icon and kind) that opens as a word is typed and with Control+Space, narrows while typing, and takes the highlighted entry with Enter or Tab as one step of the undo history; the focus stays in the field. `Sql.keywords()` gives the keywords the highlighter knows.
