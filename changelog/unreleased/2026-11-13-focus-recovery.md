- When the focused element disappears or can no longer take the focus, the focus no longer vanishes: it goes to the nearest element around where it was that takes the focus or holds one that does, then to the first such one in tree order, so that keys keep reaching the handlers around it. Closing the active tab from its content puts the focus into the content of the tab that takes over. Only when nothing below the root around it can take the focus is it cleared, as before.
