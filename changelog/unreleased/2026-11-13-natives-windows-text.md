- `rethinkj-ui-natives-windows-x64` also carries FreeType 2.15.0 and HarfBuzz 15.1.0, built from source with the options of the Linux JAR, for the text rendering to come; nothing calls them yet. They link the C runtime statically and need nothing but `KERNEL32.dll`, so no Visual C++ Redistributable is needed; the JAR is about 1.1 MB larger. FreeType comes under the FreeType License, which asks an app that ships it to credit the FreeType Project in its documentation; the licence texts are in the JAR under `META-INF/rethinkj-ui/natives/windows-x64/licenses/`. Building the module from source now needs Visual Studio 2022 or later, or its Build Tools, with the workload "Desktop development with C++".
