- **Breaking:** `Theme.Type` has a seventh style, `small` (12 px regular), for secondary text beside what it is about; a theme built with `new Theme.Type(...)` passes it as the last argument. `Label.Variant.SMALL` sets muted text in it. The status bar's parts, a completion's kind and the find bar's count use it instead of the 13 px body, so they give way to what they accompany; the count's minimum width is 48 px instead of 72 and grows with a longer count.
