- A window that follows the pointer during a drag (`Windows.Handle.moveTo` while the button is held) now keeps up with it on X11 and Windows: the loop reads the system's pointer while such a window reports it or is the pressed one, since SDL drops a move that leaves the pointer where it was in that window and the window manager confirms a move late. During a press the keys that another window reports go to the window the press began in, so Escape gives up a drag that opened a window under the pointer.
