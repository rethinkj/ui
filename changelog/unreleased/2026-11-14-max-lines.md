- `El.maxLines(n)` shows at most `n` lines of a text that wraps; with `ellipsis()` the last ends in `…` where the text goes on, and the tree dump keeps the whole string. A single-line field that wraps (`editable(…).wrapText()`) is now as high as its lines, up to that limit, instead of one line, so a read-only value can wrap in place and still be selected and copied.
