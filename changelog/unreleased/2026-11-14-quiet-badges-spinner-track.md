- Quiet badges in `Theme.DARK` are quieter: their tints were a loud step above a card, since translucent colours blend in linear light, and now sit about 1.2:1 off it (the neutral one renders `#27282A` on a card), so that the text carries the kind. A busy primary button's spinner draws its ring in the new `Theme.Borders.trackOnAccent`, white at 12 %, instead of the pressed accent, which made the ring heavy; the five-argument `Borders` constructor sets it.
