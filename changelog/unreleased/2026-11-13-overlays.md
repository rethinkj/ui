- Added overlays: `El.overlay(content)` shows a tree above everything else, beside the element it is attached to, outside the clips of its scroll areas, for menus, tooltips, dropdown lists and dialogs. The overlay is placed after layout with `side(Side, align, gap)`, `atPoint(x, y)` or `centerInWindow()`, flips to the other side or moves into the window where it does not fit, follows its anchor when it scrolls and is drawn in the order the overlays opened. A press outside calls its `onDismiss` handler and goes no further, the wheel outside dismisses and scrolls, `modal()` keeps the pointer, the wheel, the focus and the keys inside it and `scrim(argb)` darkens the window under it. An overlay that opens takes the focus and gives it back when it closes; keys it does not take go on to its anchor. Tree dumps list overlays after the tree, each with what it is anchored to.
