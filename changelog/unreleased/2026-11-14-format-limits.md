- `Format.choice(values, label)` refuses two values that are not equal with one label, with an `IllegalArgumentException` that names the label and both values; before, the label read as the first of them, silently another value. `Format.DECIMAL` refuses a number whose exponent would write more than `Format.MAX_ZEROS` (4096) zeros, such as `1e5000`, in `parse` and `allows`; before, `1e2147483647` was accepted and writing it again threw `OutOfMemoryError`.
