- Added: work off the app's thread can report progress. `App.background(name, progress -> result, onProgress, onResult, onFailure)` hands the computation a `Work.Progress`, whose `report(value)` runs `onProgress` on the app's thread; reports that wait are coalesced into the newest, and none runs after `work.cancel()` or after the result. A script shows work half done with the new step `await progress`, a test with `App.awaitProgress(Duration)`.
