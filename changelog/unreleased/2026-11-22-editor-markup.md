- `CodeEditor.State.markup(layer, text, marks)` shows the marks of several providers together, one layer each: `Diagnostic`, `Marker` and the new `Highlight`, computed for a given text. Marks move with edits elsewhere in the text, also with each change of a Replace All or a format, and go when their own characters change. `diagnostics(text, list)` is now the layer `"diagnostics"`, so diagnostics stay after an edit elsewhere instead of going, and folds now survive Replace All and Ctrl+Alt+L.
