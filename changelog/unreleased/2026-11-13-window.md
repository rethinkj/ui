- Added windows in the new package `com.rethinkj.ui.window`. `Window` opens a native window through SDL 3, with or without the system's frame, delivers its input as `WindowEvent`s (pointer, wheel, keys with stable names and modifiers, text, size, scale, close requests, and redraws while the user moves or resizes it on Windows) and gives the handles a GPU surface needs (`Window.handle()`); `Window.progress` shows progress on the taskbar. `WindowLoop` runs an `App` in a window and calls its presenter only when there is a new frame, so an idle window does nothing. A frameless window draws its own title bar: elements marked with `El.windowPart(WindowPart.DRAG)` move and snap the window, the margin along its edges resizes it, and the minimize, maximize and close parts act when clicked; `App.windowPart` reports the part at a point. On Windows 11 such a window has rounded corners and the system's shadow, shows the snap layouts on its maximize part and the system menu on a right click on a drag area.
