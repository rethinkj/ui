- Added `Menu` to the kit: items with shortcuts, check marks, disabled items, separators and one level of submenus, in an overlay, as a dropdown below a button (`Menu.dropdown`) or as a context menu at a right press (`Menu.contextMenu`). The app keeps a `Menu.State` per menu; the menu follows the pointer, opens submenus after `Theme.Motion.submenuDelay`, and takes Up, Down, Home, End, Enter, Space, Arrow Right and Left, Escape, Tab and letters that jump to items.
