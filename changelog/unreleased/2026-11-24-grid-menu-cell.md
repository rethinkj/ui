- `DataGrid.Options.contextMenu(Menu.State, DataGrid.CellMenu)` builds custom context-menu items for the cell the menu opened on: the pressed cell, or with the Menu key the cell that holds the focus; the pressed row with column `-1` on a row number; otherwise the cursor's cell; `-1, -1` without `select`. The menu closes when that row moves or is gone, and the built-in `Column` submenu follows the pressed column.
