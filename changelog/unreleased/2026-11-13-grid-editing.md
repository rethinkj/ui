- The data grid edits cells itself: `DataGrid.options().onCommit(...)` with `Column.editable()` lays an editor over the cell (Enter, F2, a double press or typing), with editors for text, numbers, booleans (Space), enumerations (`Column.choices`), dates and times, a field of several lines (Alt+Enter) and the app's own (`Column.editor`); `Column.nullable()`, Control+0 and Delete set NULL. `onPaste`, `onInsertRow`, `onDeleteRows`, `appendRow()`, `status(...)` and `history(...)` paste tab-separated text (`DataGrid.parseText`), add and mark rows and show changed, new, deleted and failed rows; `GridChanges` keeps the changes, with undo and the list to write (`options().changes(changes)`). `Theme.Grid` has the tints `changed`, `added`, `deleted` and `error`; `Column` has the components `type` and `editing`.
