- The kit has `Progress`: a bar with a value that follows it with a spring and can show an error, done or paused, a bar that runs while the length of the work is not known and springs to its value once that is known, a thin line for the edge of a card, a bar with its name and a text such as "12.4 MB of 80 MB", and a spinner for buttons, tabs and rows. `Button.button(..., busy, onPress)` shows the spinner and takes no press while its action runs, and a tree dump says `busy`.
