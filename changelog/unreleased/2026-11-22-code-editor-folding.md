- `CodeEditor` folds code: a chevron in the gutter, shown on hover and always on folded lines, folds or unfolds the bracket pair that starts on a line (outside strings and comments with a highlighter such as `Json` or `Sql`), Ctrl+Shift+[ and Ctrl+Shift+] (`Command.FOLD`, `Command.UNFOLD`) fold and unfold at the caret, and `CodeEditor.State#folds` saves and restores the folded ranges, which follow edits and go when an edit removes their brackets. `El#bracketFolds` gives the pairs that can fold of any multi-line field.
