- A focused read-only text field without a selection no longer redraws the window every 500 ms for a caret it does not show, so the app goes idle after a click on read-only text. Home and End, alone or with Control, now scroll the scroll area around the focus to its start or end, as Page Up and Page Down scroll it by a page. In a read-only field they no longer move a hidden caret, but scroll the field or the area around it.
