- Added `El.dismissGroup(group)`: a press or the wheel on an element in the same group as an open overlay is not outside it, so the overlay stays and the press goes on to the element, as a menu bar needs to switch menus with one click. `El.keepFocus()` makes a press inside an element leave the keyboard focus where it is (except in a text field) and an overlay open without taking the focus; Tab still reaches it. `El.holdFocus()` makes an element hold the focus while the view marks it, as an open overlay does, and give it back when unmarked; a press outside calls its `onDismiss` handler. `El.underline(from, to)` underlines characters of a text, as menus mark their mnemonics. Submenus that open inside a submenu that flipped at the window's edge go on in the same direction.
