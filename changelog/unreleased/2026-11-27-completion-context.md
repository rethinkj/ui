- `Completion.completeWithContext` and `Completion.completeWithContextInBackground` pass a `Completion.Context` (text snapshot, caret, word bounds, `Completion.Trigger`, and trigger character) to completion providers, and `Completion.Entry` gains `detail` (shown muted after the text), an explicit replace range (`from`/`to`), and `additionalEdits` applied with the insertion as one undo step.
