- Tabs move, tear off and dock: `Tabs.tabs(..., Tabs.options()...)` takes `onMove` (dragging along the bar with a marker, Control+Shift+Page Up and Page Down), `drag(Tabs.Dragging)`, `onTearOff`, `onDock` and a `contextMenu`; without them the tabs behave as before. `TabGroups<T>` keeps groups of tabs in several windows for an app: a tab dragged out of its bar opens in a window that follows the pointer, a window's only tab drags the window, a drop on any group's bar docks it there, the tab menu moves tabs to a new window and back, and closing a window docks or closes its tabs. The example `Documents` shows it.
