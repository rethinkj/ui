- Drags know the screen: each `Drag` step has the pointer's screen position, whether it is outside its window and which window lies under it. `El.dragPayload(object)` and `El.onDrop(handler)` let a drag carry an object to drop targets in its own window or another, with `Drop.Phase.OVER`, `LEAVE` and `DROP`; such a drag goes on when its element leaves the tree, so a card or tab can tear off into a window that follows the pointer (not on Wayland, where no client places its windows).
