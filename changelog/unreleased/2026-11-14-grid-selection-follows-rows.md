- Fixed: after the app sorted, filtered, inserted or removed rows of a `DataGrid` with `options().changes(...)`, the selection stayed on the old row positions, so Delete, Control+Minus, Control+0 and a paste changed other rows than the user selected. The cursor, the anchor and the ranges now follow their rows by key; a selection of more than `Selection.FOLLOWED_ROWS` (10 000) rows collapses to the cursor when its rows move. A grid without keys keeps its selection on its positions as before.
