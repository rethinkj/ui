- `App.lane(name)` makes a `Lane` on which background work runs one piece after another, each beginning once the one before it has ended, also when it was cancelled, as the calls on one database connection need; `Work.onEnd(handler)` tells the app when a computation has really returned, also after a cancel, `Work.isRunning()` whether it still runs, and `App.await` and a script's `await` wait for a lane's pieces that have not begun and for cancelled work with an `onEnd` handler.
