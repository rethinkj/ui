- Completions and code editors do more of what an editor with errors and suggestions needs. `Completion.State.openAfter(".")` opens the list right after the characters it names; `Completion.completeInBackground` finds entries on another thread and shows a row "Loading…" meanwhile; `Completion.Entry` takes a fourth text, the one put in place of the word, so a name that needs quotes is listed without them; and the list widens to its entries, up to twice a field's width, ending a longer text with an ellipsis. `CodeEditor.State` has `select(caret, anchor)`, which works while the editor is not shown, and `errorFrom()` and `errorTo()`, both built on the new `FieldState.select`. A tree dump shows what a field's decorator underlines, such as the editor's error, as `underline <from>-<to>`.
