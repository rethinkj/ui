- `TabGroups.accept(Function<Object, T>)` now lets apps accept drags from elsewhere in the window (such as sidebar rows with `El.dragPayload`) onto any group's tab bar or, with `splits(true)`, onto a group's center or split edges across open windows, showing the same insertion marker and drop-zone preview, moving an already-open tab when returned by identity, and leaving content's own `onDrop` targets in precedence.
