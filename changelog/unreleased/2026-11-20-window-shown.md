- `Windows.Handle.onShown(handler)` runs once a window has shown its first frame on the screen, on the UI thread in the window's next frame, so an app can tell an updater that a new version runs, close a splash screen or measure its start without a presenter of its own. A minimized or hidden window, whose frame was not shown, does not run it yet. In a script it runs at the `frame` step after the window's first.
