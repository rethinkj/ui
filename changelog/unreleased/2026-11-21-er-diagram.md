- The diagram module draws entity relationship diagrams: `ErDiagram.erDiagram(state, schemas, onPins)` shows the tables of plain `Schema`, `Table`, `Column` and `ForeignKey` records, which an app fills from `DatabaseMetaData`, as cards in schema frames with crow's-foot edges, a selection that dims unrelated tables, three table modes, a filter, levels of detail when zoomed out, keyboard navigation and a layered arrangement that keeps the tables the user pinned. The canvas now pans with the wheel and zooms around the pointer with Ctrl+wheel, lays areas under its nodes (`Canvas.canvas(viewport, areas, nodes, edges, content, onMove)`) and reveals a node (`Viewport.reveal`).
