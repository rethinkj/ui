- `Canvas.png(nodes, viewport -> canvas(viewport, …))` returns the whole diagram as PNG bytes, not only the part a canvas shows: cropped to the nodes with a small margin, at 100 % and a scale of 2, in the current theme, drawn without a window. A diagram with more than 16 megapixels at a scale of 2 is saved at 1; one too large at 1 as well is refused with an `IllegalArgumentException` before anything is drawn.
