- Added `TextField.textArea(theme, value, placeholder, onChange)`, a multi-line field in the frame of the text field that wraps its lines and is four lines high, and `TextField.codeArea(...)`, the same in the mono type without wrapping, in which Tab indents by four spaces; both have the theme's scrollbars and take `.lines(n)`, `.wrapText(boolean)`, `.readOnly()` and a null handler to disable them.
