- `CodeEditor` now supports rendered doc comments (reader mode) for `Language` highlighters: enable with `CodeEditor.State#readerMode(true)` (off by default) and receive link clicks with `CodeEditor.State#onLink(handler)`; toggle individual comments with the gutter button or `CodeEditor.run(app, state, CodeEditor.Command.TOGGLE_DOC_COMMENT)`. `Markdown.Options#textColor(int)` overrides prose text color in `Markdown` fragments and definition lists.
