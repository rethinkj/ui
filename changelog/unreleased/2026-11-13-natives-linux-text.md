- `rethinkj-ui-natives-linux-x64` also carries FreeType 2.15.0 and HarfBuzz 15.1.0, for the text rendering to come; nothing calls them yet. Both are built from source without dependencies beyond the C runtime, which makes the JAR about 1.4 MB larger. FreeType comes under the FreeType License, which asks an app that ships it to credit the FreeType Project in its documentation; the licence texts are in the JAR under `META-INF/rethinkj-ui/natives/linux-x64/licenses/`. Building the module from source now needs a C++ compiler as well.
