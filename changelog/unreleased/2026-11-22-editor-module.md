- `CodeEditor` moved from `rethinkj-ui-kit` to the new module `rethinkj-ui-editor`, package `com.rethinkj.ui.editor`: add that dependency and change the import. `Completion` and `Find` stay in kit and work on any multi-line field. New in kit for components like the editor: `Find.find` with the keys the field runs as commands of its own, `Find.next` for the next occurrence of a text, `TextField.menu` for a field's menu with items of the app's after its own, `Tooltip.panel` for a tooltip's panel shown where the view decides, and the constant `Completion.ROWS`.
