- `Button.options().subtitle(text)` and `subtitle(text, layout)` add a secondary detail to `Button.button`, `Button.menu`, and `Button.split`, either `Button.Subtitle.INLINE` (default, muted on the same line keeping the button's height) or `Button.Subtitle.STACKED` (smaller below the title, growing the button to fit both lines). The tree-dump label and tooltips combine title and subtitle, and a narrow view ellipsizes the subtitle before the title. A split button that its view makes narrower now cuts its main half's text instead of pushing its chevron out.
