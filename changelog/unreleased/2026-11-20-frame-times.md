- Measure a real window's frames: `WindowLoop.frames(listener)` tells each frame a window shows as a `WindowLoop.Frame`, in nanoseconds of input, build, layout, draw and present and the time since the frame before, and `Window.post(event)` hands a window an event as if the system had reported it, so that a test can feed it wheel steps one per frame. `App.timing()` gives the input, build and layout of the last `frame()` without a window, and a `WindowLoop.Presenter` may tell how much of a present was drawing (`drawNanos()`). See the page Frame times under Testing.
