- Added `Chat` to the kit for chat messages: `Chat.user` shows the user's text in a bubble at the end side, as wide as the text up to `Theme.Chat.bubbleWidth()`; `Chat.agent` shows the answer as Markdown over the full width; `Chat.tool` is a one-line tool call (running, done, failed) that opens to its details; `Chat.working` is a spinner line. A message can have a copy action and a muted time under it.
