- `Tabs.Options.overflow(Tabs.Overflow)` shows a trailing icon button (`#overflow`, label from `Texts.allTabs()`, `"All tabs"` / `"Alle Tabs"`) whenever the tabs do not fit in the bar; it opens a dropdown listing every tab in bar order with the active one checked, and choosing a tab selects it and scrolls it into view even if it is already active. `TabGroups` enables it automatically for every group.
