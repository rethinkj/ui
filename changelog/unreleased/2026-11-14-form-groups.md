- `Form.form` takes groups: `Form.group(title, entries...)` puts fields under a heading (`role=group`, with `.description(text)`, `.divided()` and `.level(n)`), and the label column is one for the whole form, as wide as the widest label of any group. `Form.labelWidth(theme, entries...)` and `Form.form(theme, labels, labelWidth, entries...)` line up the labels of forms in separate cards. `form` now takes `Form.Entry...`; calls with fields compile as before.
