- Find over a selection scope scrolls a list that builds only the columns in view sideways to a match in a column out of view and shows it, and after rows are inserted above the current match, Enter goes on to the next match instead of selecting the same one again. For this, `El.onSelect` also works on an `El.selectionScope()`: it is told the scope's selection as indexes into `App.scopeText`, also when rows inserted above move it.
