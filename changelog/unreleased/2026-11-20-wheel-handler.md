- `El.onWheel((deltaX, deltaY, modifiers, x, y) -> …)` lets an element handle the wheel itself, with the modifiers held and the pointer's position in the element, and take it by returning `true`, as a canvas zooms with Ctrl+wheel; a wheel that no handler takes scrolls as before. `App.wheel` takes the modifiers too, `WindowEvent.Wheel` carries them from the window, and the script step `wheel` accepts `shift`, `control`, `alt` and `meta` after the distances. A record pattern that takes `WindowEvent.Wheel` apart now names its five components.
