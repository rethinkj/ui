- On Windows, keys typed right after a click or Tab into a text field, or after the key that opens a dialog whose field takes the focus, are no longer lost when they arrive together with it, as from a fast typist or a tool that types: their characters, as the keyboard layout makes them with dead keys and AltGr, reach the field (`WindowEvent.KeyText`, `App.keyText`), while the character of a key that pressed a button stays out.
