- `DataGrid.table(rows)` makes a `GridTable<T>` over rows of records or POJOs, whose `column(title, width, format, getter[, wither | setter])` makes typed columns that take their look and editor from the `Format`; `Options.onCommit(table, (row, value) -> error)` hands the app the row with the typed value, `table.comparator` and `table.filter` sort and filter the app's rows by typed values, `Filter.matches(value, format)` tests one, `GridChanges.changes(table, columns)` lists the pending changes with typed values and `apply(row)`, and `Column.cell(fn)` changes a column's text keeping its typed part.
