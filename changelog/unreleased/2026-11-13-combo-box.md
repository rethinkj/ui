- `ComboBox.comboBox` chooses among many choices by typing: closed it looks like a `Select`, open it is a field whose text filters the choices fuzzily, the matched characters in a heavier weight. `ComboBox.free` takes any text and suggests values as one types. Both build on `FilterList`, a filtered list of entries with groups, icons, details and shortcuts whose entries the kit filters with `Fuzzy` (100 000 entries in a few milliseconds per key) or the app finds itself, also on another thread. `Theme.Sizes` has a new last component, `palette`.
