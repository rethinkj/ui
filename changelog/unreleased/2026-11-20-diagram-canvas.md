- The new optional module `rethinkj-ui-diagram` (package `com.rethinkj.ui.diagram`) starts with `Canvas.canvas`: nodes at diagram coordinates on an area that pans by dragging empty space and zooms in steps from 25 % to 200 % with `+`, `-` and `0` (fit), laid out again at each zoom so that text stays sharp. Nodes outside the canvas are not built, a node's content gets the zoom to show less when zoomed out, and a dragged node is reported moved and pinned. Its texts are in `DiagramTexts`, with `GermanDiagramTexts`.
