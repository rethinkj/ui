- Added `Drawer` to the kit: a modal or non-modal side sheet anchored to a window edge (`Side.RIGHT`, `LEFT`, `TOP` or `BOTTOM`) with a title, close button and scrollable content, sized by the new `Theme.Sizes.drawer` token (default 360) unless its content sets a pixel width. Core's `El.atWindowEdge(side, align, gap)` now supports `Style.Align.STRETCH` to stretch an overlay along the whole edge less `gap` at both ends.
