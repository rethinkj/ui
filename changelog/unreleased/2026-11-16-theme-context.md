- **Breaking:** Kit components no longer take a `Theme` as their first parameter. They read it from `Theme.CURRENT`, which is `Theme.DARK` unless the app provides another: `app.provide(Theme.CURRENT, Settings::theme)` gives the whole view the theme its state chooses, and `Theme.CURRENT.with(theme, () -> part)` gives one to a part of the view. Drop the theme argument from every kit call, and read tokens in the view with `Theme.CURRENT.get()`. The leading parts of `Menu.Item`, `FilterList.Entry` and `ComboBox.Item` and the look of `Radio.option` are now `Supplier<El>`. Core's new `Context` carries such a value for any app: `App.provide` binds it for each build of the view, `Context.with` for a part, and the rows of virtual lists get the value that held where their list was made.
