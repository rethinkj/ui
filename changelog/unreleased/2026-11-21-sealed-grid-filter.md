- **Breaking:** `DataGrid.Filter` is now a column and a sealed `Spec`: `TextMatch(kind, value)`, `RegexMatch(expression)`, `Between(from, to)`, `OneOf(values)` or `NullCheck(isNull)`, built as `new Filter(column, new Filter.Between("10", "100"))`. The factories `Filter.of`, `Filter.between` and `Filter.oneOf` and the accessors `kind()`, `value()`, `to()` and `values()` are gone; switch over `filter.spec()` instead. A `RegexMatch` compiles its expression once instead of once per cell, and throws `PatternSyntaxException` when it is made from an invalid expression, where the old filter matched nothing.
