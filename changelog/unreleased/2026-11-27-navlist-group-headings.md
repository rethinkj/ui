- `NavList` headings gain a leading element, always-visible actions, and a group-header style: `Heading.leading(element)` places a passive (`inert`) element such as an `Avatar` before the label, `Heading.actions(buttons...)` places icon-sized action buttons, such as `Button.icon`, at the right end as siblings of the collapse toggle (so pressing one never toggles, Tab moves from the toggle to its actions, and a disabled list leaves them disabled and inert), and `NavList.group(label)` draws a heading on a quiet filled surface with `Label.Variant.STRONG` text and a far-right chevron. The record `NavList.Heading` has new components.
