- `Theme.LIGHT`'s surfaces are now distinct tones instead of one white: the window `#D7DBE2`, cards (`panel`) `#FAFBFC`, bands and a grid's header `#EAEDF1`, hairlines (`border().subtle()`) `#D9DDE3`, while controls and popups (`raised`) stay white. To hold its contrast rules on the darker window, the light theme's hover and pressed overlays (now a mid grey at 20 and 34 %), muted and disabled text, the strong border and its track, the scrollbar's thumb, the inactive selection, links, a quiet accent badge's text and a dangerous button's text are a little darker. An app that wants the old surfaces back writes `Theme.LIGHT.withSurface(new Theme.Surfaces(rgb(0xECEEF2), WHITE, WHITE, rgb(0xF7F8FA), alpha(rgb(0xBDBFC5), 0.3f), alpha(rgb(0xBDBFC5), 0.5f), alpha(BLACK, 0.35f)))` with `Colors`' `rgb`, `alpha`, `WHITE` and `BLACK`, and `withBorder` and `withGrid` for the hairline `#E6E8EC` and the grid's header `#F7F8FA`. `Palette` gained the new colours and lost those no default theme uses any more.
