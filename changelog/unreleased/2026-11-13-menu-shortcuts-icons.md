- Breaking: menu items act on their key combinations. `Menu.Item.shortcut(modifiers, key)` declares one, shown as the platform writes it (`Ctrl+Shift+N`, `Menu.shortcutText`), and `Menu.shortcuts(items)` or `MenuBar.keys` make a key handler for the root that runs the enabled item when no element on the focus path took the key, so a text field keeps Control+C; two items with one combination are refused with an exception that names both. `Menu.Item` gained the components `keys` and `icon`; `Menu.Item.icon(Symbol)` shows an icon before the text, check marks and submenu arrows are icons (`Menu.CHECKED` and `Menu.SUBMENU` are `Symbol`s, as is `Select.OPENS`), and submenus nest to any depth, with `Menu.State.active(level)` and `submenu(level)` for each level.
