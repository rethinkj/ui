- Added keyboard input and focus. `App.keyDown`, `App.keyUp` and `App.textInput` take keys (the new `Key` record: web key name, modifiers, repeat) and text, and `WindowLoop` passes them on from the window. `El.focusable()` marks an element that takes the focus, which Tab and Shift+Tab move in tree order and a pointer press gives; `App.focused()` and `App.focus(el)` read and set it, and `App` keeps it across rebuilds by the element's key or position. Keys go to `El.onKey` (and `El.onKeyUp`) of the focused element and then of its ancestors until a handler returns true; text goes to `El.onText` of the focused element. Enter and Space press a focused element that has `onPress`. Focus moved by the keyboard or by `App.focus` scrolls the element into view and draws a 2-pixel ring around it as an ordinary `Scene` instance (`Scene.of(root, ring, colour)`), in a colour `App.focusRing` changes. `El.dump()` ends the focused element's line with `focused`, and `Script` has the steps `key <name> [shift] [control] [alt] [meta]`, `type "<text>"` and `focus <target>`. Breaking: `WindowEvent.KeyDown` and `KeyUp` now carry a `Key`, and the modifier bits moved from `WindowEvent` to `Key`.
