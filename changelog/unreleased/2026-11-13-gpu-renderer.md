- Added the GPU renderer. `GpuRenderer` draws a `Scene` through wgpu with the formulas of `CpuRenderer`, blending in linear light into an sRGB target; its images match the CPU renderer's within a step or two of 8 bits per channel. `GpuRenderer.create()` and `render(scene, width, height, scale)` draw into `Pixels` without a window. `GpuPresenter` shows the frames of a `WindowLoop` in a `Window`, on Direct3D 12 on Windows and Vulkan on Linux: `new WindowLoop(window, app, new GpuPresenter(window))`. A `WindowLoop.Presenter` can now report a frame it could not show yet with `pending()`, and the loop offers the newest frame again after a refresh of the display.
