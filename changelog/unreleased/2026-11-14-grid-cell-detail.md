- `DataGrid.Column.detail(row -> text)` shows a short muted text beside a cell's value, such as the size of a long value, a unit or a count: after the value in a column aligned left, before it in one aligned right. It keeps its width while the value ends in an ellipsis and is left out where the cell has no room for it and the value's first character; the tooltip then names both. Copies and the editor take the value alone, and a tree dump says the detail as the cell's `value`. Typed columns of a `GridTable` take it the same way.
