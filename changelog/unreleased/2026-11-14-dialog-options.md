- `Dialog.dialog(theme, title, content, buttons, onDismiss, Dialog.options()...)` takes an element before the title (`leading`, as a colour mark), elements at the end of its head (`trailing`, as a badge or a close button), elements at the start of its button row (`start`, as Test connection at the left of Cancel and Save) and the default button. A long title ends in an ellipsis with the whole in a tooltip, and is now `role=heading` at level 2. The head comes after the content and the buttons in the order of Tab, so a dialog still opens in its content's first field.
