- **Breaking:** A section's heading on a page is larger, in the new `Theme.Type.section` (16, semibold), so that it ranks between a page's title (20) and a card's (14); `Label.Variant.SECTION` shows text in it. A theme that builds its own `Theme.Type` passes the new style as its last component. A page's first section is closer to its title (`space().xl()`), and the headings of pages and sections wrap instead of running past the edge.
