- Added `MenuBar.menuBar(theme, state, menus)` to the kit: a row of titles, each a `Menu.submenu("&File", items)` whose `&` marks the mnemonic, opening its menu below it. While a menu is open, a press on another title or moving the pointer onto it switches menus; Alt alone or F10 gives the bar the keys, Alt with a mnemonic opens its menu, Left and Right move between menus, and Escape steps back one level until the focus is back where it was. The titles take presses inside a title bar marked `El.windowPart(DRAG)`, whose space between them still moves the window. The root takes `MenuBar.keys(state, menus)` and `MenuBar.keysUp(state, menus)`.
