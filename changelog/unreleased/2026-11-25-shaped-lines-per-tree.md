- A wrapping text field of more than 4,096 lines, such as a sent 8,000-line log in a chat, no longer shapes its lines several times in every rebuild: a field keeps up to 16,384 shaped lines or about a million characters while a view is built, instead of 4,096 lines. Typing next to a sent 8,000-line message costs about twice what it costs next to a 4,000-line one, instead of four times.
