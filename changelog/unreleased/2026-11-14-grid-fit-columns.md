- `DataGrid.options().fitColumns(fit)` makes every column of a data grid as wide as its header and the cells of the rows that exist, once per result, within the theme's minimum and maximum column width, so that a table opens with its headers and values whole. The grid tells the widths to `onResize`, all in one handler, and the app keeps them as for a resize by hand; with `loading` the fit waits for the first page. The app keeps a `DataGrid.Fit` and calls its `reset()` for a new result. Columns resized by hand before the fit are left alone, and a hidden column is fitted when it is first shown.
