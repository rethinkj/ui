- Added: `Select.select`, `ComboBox.comboBox`, `ComboBox.items`, `Radio.radioGroup`, `Segmented.segmented` and `NavList.navList` take values of any type besides texts and indexes: the list of values, the selected value (found by `equals`, null for none), a function to its text or item and a handler that gets the value, as in `select(t, List.of(Level.values()), s.level, Level::label, s.levelMenu, v -> s.level = v)`. `NavList.navList` can also put a heading over each group of values. The forms with indexes stay as they are.
