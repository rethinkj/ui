- `Split.Position` can now collapse and restore either `Split.Part` (`FIRST` or `SECOND`) via `collapse(Part)`, `restore()`, and `isCollapsed()`, keeping `kind` and `value` so restoring returns to the last open position. A collapsed part takes zero size and leaves the tree (removing the gap in `Split.cards` too), and leaves the divider at the edge where double-clicking, pressing Enter, dragging, or pressing an arrow key away restores it; passing a collapsed `reset` makes double-click and Enter toggle collapse while keeping the current position.
