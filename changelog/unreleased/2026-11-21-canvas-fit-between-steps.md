- `Canvas.Viewport.fit()` now chooses the largest zoom in the range of `Viewport.ZOOMS` (25 % to 200 %) at which all nodes fit with the margin, including zooms between steps; `zoomBy`, `zoomIn`, `zoomOut`, `+`/`-` and Ctrl+wheel move from a zoom between steps to the next step in that direction. `ErStyle.DEFAULT` lowers its `skeleton` threshold from `0.75f` to `0.6f`, so fitted entity relationship diagrams keep readable column names down to 60 %.
