- `App.after(seconds, action)` runs an action on the app's thread once the app's clock has moved on by that much, in the first frame at or after that time, so that an app can change its state later without input: a delay, a timeout, data that arrives after a while. `App.nextChange()` includes it, so a window wakes for it, and in a script `advance` and `frame` run it.
