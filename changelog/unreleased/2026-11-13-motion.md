- Added `El.motion(Spring)`: when a rebuild gives such an element a new rectangle, fill, border colour or radius, `App` springs it there instead of changing it at once, without running the view again. Elements are matched across rebuilds by key, or by position among their siblings. `App.advance` moves the app's clock, `App.isMoving` tells whether anything is still moving, and `App.frame` returns false once everything is at rest. Siblings with the same key are now rejected. `Track.target` returns a track's target.
