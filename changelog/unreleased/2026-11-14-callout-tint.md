- `Callout` is now a tinted box of its kind's colour with a rounded bar along its leading edge, instead of the raised surface inside a thin coloured edge, so a note stands out from the content around it; `INFO` is blue instead of grey. Its colours are the new token group `Theme.Callouts` (`theme.callout()`, `withCallout`), a `CalloutTone` per kind with the fill and the colour of the bar and icon, the colour of the text below the title and the bar's width; a theme made with a constructor without it gets `Callouts.DARK` or `Callouts.LIGHT` by its lightness. The callout's padding at its trailing edge is now 8 instead of 12, and in a tree dump its first child is the bar, an element without a role.
