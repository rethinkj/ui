- `Markdown.Options.scroll(ScrollState)` gives a `Markdown.markdown` document a `ScrollState` of its own, `App.scrollToRow(ScrollState, int)` scrolls that list to a row such as `Markdown.rowOf(document, id)` without searching the element tree, and `ScrollState.topRow()` with `Markdown.current(document, headings, scroll)` reports the heading at the top of the document and rebuilds the view as scrolling moves it.
