- The kit's `CodeEditor` finds and replaces: `codeEditor(theme, app, …)` has a find bar over its top right corner (`Find`, which `Find.find(theme, app, field, state)` also gives a `TextField.textArea`) with Control+F, Control+H, F3 and Shift+F3, switches for case, whole words, regular expressions and the selection, a count of the matches, and Replace all as one step of the undo history; long texts are searched on another thread. It also goes to a line (Control+G), marks the other occurrences of the word at the caret, and has Control+D, Control+Shift+D, Alt+Up and Alt+Down, Control+Shift+K and Control+/ (`CodeEditor.Command`). `Theme.Code` has the backgrounds `match`, `current` and `occurrence`; `App.select` now scrolls a multi-line field that does not have the focus, and `El.onSelect()` and `El.decorator()` return what the view gave.
