- A text field's `onSelect` handler hears the selection after an edit (typed text, `App.edit`, `FieldState` edits, undo and redo) only once the app has answered it, and then after every edit, also when the selection stayed. Its positions always fit the app's string; before, it briefly heard positions in a refused edit's text. A field with `onSelect` now builds its view once more per edit, and the build that answers an edit still sees the caret the handler stored before it.
