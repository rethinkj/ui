- Added multi-line text fields: `El.text(value).editable(onEdit).multiline()`. The string holds a line feed per line break (carriage returns and U+2028/U+2029 from the app, typing and pasting become line feeds), and the field scrolls as a scroll area of its own, with the wheel and with `El.scrollbars`. Enter breaks the line and Control+Enter goes on to the `onKey` handlers; Up and Down move by rows on screen and keep their column, Page Up and Page Down by the rows in view, Home goes to the start of the row and a second Home to the indent, Control+Home and Control+End to the ends; a third press selects a line with its line feed, a fourth everything. `wrapText(boolean)` wraps the lines at the field's width or lets the field scroll sideways, `lines(n)` makes it n lines high when its style gives no height, `tabSize(n)` sets the tab stops, and `tabIndents(spaces)` makes Tab and Shift+Tab indent and outdent instead of moving the focus (Escape and then Tab, or Control+M, leave). Only the rows in view are shaped and laid out, so a text of 100 000 lines costs a frame what the lines in view cost; an edit still hands the app a new string. Masked fields cannot be multi-line.
- The undo history of every text field now keeps what each edit replaced instead of a copy of the whole string per step.
- The tree dump writes a text's string whole up to 200 characters and a longer one as its first 100 and its last 40 with its length (`"…" … "…" length 104857`), also in `Script`'s errors, and writes a tab as `\t`, which `text="..."` and `type "..."` read. A multi-line field adds the lines in view (`lines 12-20/100000`) and the caret's line and column (`caret 57 (3:12)`).
