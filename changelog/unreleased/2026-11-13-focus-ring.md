- Added `El.focusRing(argb)`: an element states the colour of its own focus ring, and `app.focusRing(argb)` is the default for elements that state none. The kit's focusable components (button, toggle, checkbox, text field, data grid) take it from `theme.border().focus()`, so apps no longer call `app.focusRing`, and a view that switches its theme, or shows two at once, gets the right ring.
