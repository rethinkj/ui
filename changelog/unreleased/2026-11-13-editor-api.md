- Multi-line fields can have a gutter of line numbers or markers (`El.gutter`), built for the lines in view and scrolling with the text. A field tells the app its caret and selection (`El.onSelect`), `App.edit` replaces a range and places the caret as one undo step that goes through the field's edit handler, `App.select` selects, and an overlay placed with `El.atCaret()` lies at the caret, as a list of completions does.
