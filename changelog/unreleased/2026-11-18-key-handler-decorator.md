- `El.onKeyFirst(handler)` adds a key handler that is asked before the element's own, which still gets the keys it declines, and `El.alsoDecorate(decorator)` adds marks after a field's own decorator, so that they draw over its marks of the same kind. Adding Control+Enter or marks of your own to a `CodeEditor` no longer means fetching and chaining its handler or decorator by hand; `onKey` and `decorate` still replace them.
