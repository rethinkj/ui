- One `WindowLoop` runs several windows, each with an `App` of its own: handlers open and close windows through `app.windows()` (`Windows.open(title, width, height, options, app)`, `Window.Options.position(x, y)`), apps of the same state object show what another's handler changed in the same frames, the loop waits once for all and paces each window by its own display, a window's `onCloseRequest` handler may veto its closing, and the loop ends with its last window.
