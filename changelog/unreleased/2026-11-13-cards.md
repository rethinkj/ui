- Added `Card.card` in three variants (outlined, filled, elevated) that clips its content to its rounded corners, `Card.header` with a title, a description and actions, and `Card.pressable`, which makes a whole card one press target with an optional selected state. Pages and sections: `Section.page` and `Section.section` give a page of settings or a detail panel its headings (`role=heading` with `level`) and spacing. `Divider.divider` and `Divider.vertical` draw hairlines, optionally with a label.
