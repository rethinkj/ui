- **Breaking:** `Card`'s variants lie on the panel's surface, and an outlined card is lifted by `Theme.Elevation#card`. New: `Card.flush` for parts edge to edge, such as a data grid, with `Card.toolbar`, `Card.footer` and `Card.inset`; `Split.cards` for two cards side by side on the window; `Button.button` with an icon before its text and `Button.icon` with an icon alone and a tooltip; `TextField.withIcon`; `Font.sansMedium()` and `Font.sansSemibold()`. The data grid has a header band, row hairlines and no column lines, menus highlight with the selection's tint, and the tree's rows are rounded.
