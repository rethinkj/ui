- `StatusBar` is a band in the cards' color with a hairline along its top, and a short hairline between the parts and groups of each end. New: `StatusBar.group` keeps parts together, `statusBar(start, message, end)` gives a message the room between the ends (cut first when the window is narrow), `StatusBar.menu` makes a part open a menu with a chevron, `item(tone, icon, text, onPress)` tints a part as a `Badge`, and a cut text shows in a tooltip. A status shows its state by shape: `OK` a dot, `IDLE` a ring, `BUSY` a spinner, the new `WARNING` and `ERROR` a sign with the part tinted. Breaking: `StatusBar.State.CONNECTED` is now `OK`, every part or group of an end is set apart by a hairline (put parts that belong together in a `group`), and `StatusBar.height()` includes the hairline; leave a gap between cards and the bar.
