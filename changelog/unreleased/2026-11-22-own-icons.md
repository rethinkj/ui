- `Icon` is now a sealed interface implemented by `Symbol`, `Icon.Glyph` (`Icon.glyph(font, codePoint, label)`), and `Icon.Picture` (`Icon.image(image, label)`), and every kit component accepts any `Icon` wherever it took a `Symbol`; glyphs are sized and colored like symbols while pictures keep their own colors and dim to 40 % opacity when disabled. Accessors and record components that returned `Symbol` (such as `Menu.Item#icon`, `Tabs.Tab#icon`, `NavList.Item#icon`, `DataGrid.Column#icon`, `DataGrid.CellIcon#symbol`, `Breadcrumb.Crumb#icon`, `FilterList.Entry#symbol`, `Completion.Entry#symbol`, and `CodeEditor.Marker#symbol`) now return `Icon`.
