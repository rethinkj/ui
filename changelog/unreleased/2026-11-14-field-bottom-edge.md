- Fields have a quieter frame: text fields, text areas, password, number and value fields, selects, closed combo boxes and the framed code editor draw their top and sides in `border().control()` and a strong line along the bottom in `border().strong()`, which still stands out 3:1. Under the pointer the whole frame turns strong, with the focus the frame and the line take the accent color, and an invalid field keeps its frame in the danger color all round. The line is a sharp shadow one stroke below the field's box, so a field no longer has a bottom border and pads its bottom by a stroke instead; a `.shadow(...)` set on a field replaces the line. The dark theme's `border().strong()` is a step lighter (#747780), so that the line stands out 3:1 from a field's hovered fill too.
