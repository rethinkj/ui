- Fixed in layout, scroll areas and virtual lists: an aspect ratio no longer raises a height that is set or one held down by a maximum, and a limit goes through the ratio only to an axis whose size is not set, as in browsers; `space-between` without room falls back to flex-start also under `wrap-reverse` and `row-reverse`; a scroll area reaches what sticks out of a child that does not clip; a nested `fixedX` element no longer moves by the offset; rows of a fractional height no longer overlap by a pixel; a list with `RowHeights` estimated too high fills its view in the next frames instead of leaving it partly empty; `onResize` reports the size layout gave an element with motion, and reports again to an element that comes back.
