- A window draws input and tasks posted from other threads at most once per refresh of the display: what arrives less than a refresh after the last frame began waits for that refresh, together with whatever else arrives meanwhile, and what arrives after a pause is drawn at once. Fast wheel scrolling, drags and streams of tasks no longer draw a frame per event where presenting does not wait for the display, as on a machine whose display is virtual, which drew several frames per refresh that the display never showed.
