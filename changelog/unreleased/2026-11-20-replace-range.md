- `CodeEditor.State.replace(from, to, text, caret)` and `FieldState.replace(from, to, text, caret)` replace exactly the characters `from` to `to` from a handler, such as a line below the one with the caret, without selecting them first: one step of the undo history, after which Control+Z brings back the selection the user had. Unlike `App.edit`, `caret` is a position in `text`, from 0 to its length, as in `replaceSelection(text, caret)`.
