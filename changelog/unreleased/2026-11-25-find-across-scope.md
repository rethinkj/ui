- `Find.find` also takes an `El.selectionScope()`, such as `Markdown.markdown` or `Chat.transcript`: Control+F finds a text across all its read-only fields in order, counts and marks the matches, and Enter and Shift+Enter step between them, also into rows of a virtual list out of view. For this, `App.scopeText` gives a scope's text, `App.select` selects in it, and `El.decorate` marks it.
