- Forms over records and POJOs: `Editing<T>` keeps the original, the edited value, the drafts of its typed fields and their errors, with `changed()`, `valid()`, `reset()`, `saved()`, rules across fields (`rule`) and errors the app learns later (`prop.error(message)`, cleared by the next edit). A `Part` constant beside the record (`Part.of(Profile::port, Profile::withPort)`, or `Part.ofSetter` for a POJO) names each field, and `e.at(part)` gives its `Prop`, which the text, value, password and number fields, the checkbox, the toggle, the typed choices and the slider take in place of a value and a handler; `Form.field` shows the field's error, `Form.form` leaves out null fields, and `GridTable.column` takes a `Part` too. The ConnectionDialog example edits a profile record this way.
