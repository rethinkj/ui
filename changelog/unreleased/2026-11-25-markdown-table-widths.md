- Markdown table columns are no longer capped at half the prose measure: they take their natural width when the table fits, give way proportionally by their content and wrap in narrower containers, and scroll horizontally only when their longest words cannot fit. The table keeps room for the scrollbar below it only while it scrolls. Measured column widths are cached with the `MarkdownDocument` per window scale and kept across `MarkdownDocument.next(...)` updates.
