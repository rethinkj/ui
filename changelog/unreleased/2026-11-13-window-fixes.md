- Windows are more robust: a window closed right after it asked for a file dialog cancels that dialog too, instead of leaving it on the screen without an owner; a press on a frameless window's maximize part, or a right press on its drag area, ends wherever it is released, also over the content; the cursor is the one the window under the pointer asks for, whatever other windows draw; a window opened without the focus, as a torn-off tab, no longer blinks its caret; a presenter that fails to close no longer leaves the loop's other windows open, and a window closed past the loop still has its presenter closed; and the loop paces a window by its new display's refresh rate after it moved to another display or the display's mode changed (`WindowEvent.DisplayChanged`, a new event).
