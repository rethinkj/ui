- The data grid sets filters per column: `DataGrid.options().filters(filters, onFilter)` puts a funnel in each header cell (and Alt+Arrow Down on the grid) that opens a popover by the column's type — text that contains, starts with, equals or matches, a range, the values of a boolean or an enumeration, NULL or not — and hands the app a `DataGrid.Filter` per column, which it applies, in SQL or with `Filter.matches`; `DataGrid.filterBar(...)` shows them as badges to remove.
