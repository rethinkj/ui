- Tooltips show for the keyboard too: `El.whenFocusVisibleFor(seconds, look)` applies a look once an element has had the focus with its ring for that long, until the focus leaves or a key goes down, and the kit's `Tooltip.tooltip` uses it with the pointer's delay. A tooltip that would cover its own anchor, which made it flicker under the pointer, goes to a side across where one has room.
