- `Theme.Borders` has a new token `divider` for hairlines that separate content on a surface: `Divider`, a menu's separators, the lines between a `Group`'s rows, the line under a tab bar and above a card's footer, a split's line and a toolbar's separators. It is `#33363D` in `Theme.DARK`, where these lines hardly showed on a dialog's or menu's raised surface, and `#C4C8CF` in `Theme.LIGHT`; `subtle` stays for card edges and grid lines. `new Borders(subtle, strong, control, track)` still works and uses `subtle` for dividers; `withBorder` with `divider` set to `subtle` brings the old look back.
