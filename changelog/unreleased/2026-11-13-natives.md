- Added `rethinkj-ui-natives-windows-x64`, which carries SDL 3.4.20 and wgpu-native 29.0.1.1 for Windows on x64. On first use, `rethinkj-ui-core` unpacks them into a cache folder per user (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\rethinkj-ui\natives`) and loads them from there. `-Drethinkj.ui.natives.cache=<folder>` moves that cache, and `-Drethinkj.ui.natives.dir=<folder>` loads the libraries and their `manifest.txt` from a folder you ship, without unpacking anything.
