- `wrapText()` now breaks a word, path, URL or identifier that is wider than the whole line inside it, between graphemes and as late as it fits, as CSS's `overflow-wrap: break-word` does, instead of letting it run out of its box. This holds for text elements and for multi-line fields that wrap, whose carets, selection, presses and Up, Down, Home and End follow the new rows. A text still breaks between words wherever it can, and layout still gives a text in a row room for its longest word whole; only a box that is in fact narrower breaks the word.
