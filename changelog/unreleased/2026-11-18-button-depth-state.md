- `Button` has depth and one state. Primary and secondary buttons lie on a slight shadow (`Theme.Elevation.control` and `controlHover`); a press removes it, tightens the corners and moves the content down a pixel; the focus ring lies `Theme.Sizes.ringGap` apart from every button; an icon beside a secondary or ghost button's text is muted; and a primary button that is off keeps a pale tint of its fill (`Theme.Danger.disabled` for a red one). `Button.options().state(READY | BUSY | DISABLED)` replaces `busy(boolean)` and `enabled(boolean)`, and a button is busy also without a handler; `busyText("Saving…")` keeps the verb beside the spinner at a steady width; `size(SMALL | LARGE)` makes a button 20 or 32 high (`Theme.Sizes.controlSmall` and `controlLarge`); `shortcut(modifiers, key)` shows the keys after the label in its tooltip; `Button.menu` is a button that opens a menu; `Toolbar.Item.options(...)` gives a toolbar's buttons their state, tone and shortcut; a press on a button that is off no longer takes the focus from the editor it acts on; and `Button.button(variant, icon, text, busy, onPress)` is gone.
