- Layout follows the flexbox specification in five more cases: `grow` and `shrink` factors that sum to less than one no longer take the gaps off the free space twice; a container sized by its items counts an item no larger than its maximum size; an item shrinks no further than its padding and border, and the others take the rest instead of overflowing; cross-axis `auto` margins leave an item larger than its line at the line's start instead of moving it out by half the overflow; and an element with `display: none`, with everything inside it, is not placed (`El.isPlaced()` is false), so it is neither drawn, hit nor found by a selector.
