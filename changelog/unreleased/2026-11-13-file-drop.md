- `El.onFileDrop(handler)` lets an element take files that the user drags onto the window from a file manager, and text that no text field takes, with `Drop`'s phases `OVER`, `LEAVE` and `DROP` and the paths (`List<Path>`) or the text as the payload; text dropped on a text field goes in at that point as a paste. The script step `drop` drops files or text, or drags them over a target.
