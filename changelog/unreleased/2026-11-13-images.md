- Images: `El.image(Image)` shows an `Image` made with `Image.of(pixels[, scale])` or read with `Image.decode(bytes[, scale])`, sized to its pixels in logical pixels and scaled by a width or a height with its aspect ratio kept, fitted with `fit(Image.Fit.FILL | CONTAIN | COVER)`, clipped by rounded corners, with role `img` and an `image` line in the tree's dump. Both renderers sample it the same way, one to one where it fits the pixel grid and from filtered levels where it is shown smaller, so photos neither flicker nor fray; `App.imageMemory(bytes)` bounds the GPU memory images keep (256 MB unless set), and `GpuPresenter.imageUploads()` counts what went to the GPU.
