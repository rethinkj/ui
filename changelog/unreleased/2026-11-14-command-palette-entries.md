- `CommandPalette`: `FilterList.Entry.key(key)` tells entries with the same text apart, so the highlight and Enter no longer jump to the first of them when the list is built again. `Entry.keepsOpen(true)` leaves the palette open when the entry is chosen, for one that loads more entries, and the best entry is highlighted once they arrive. `State.onClose(runnable)` reports each close. A closed palette now lets go of its entries and what their handlers hold; the recently used commands stay.
