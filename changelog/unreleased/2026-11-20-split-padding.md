- `Split.split` and `Split.cards` with padding (or a border) keep the divider within their content box: dragging it to the end now stores the size the first part shows, instead of one larger by the padding that a wider window would then grow the part to. A split too small for both parts' minimums keeps its position when the divider is dragged or moved with the keys, instead of reporting one that a wider window shows differently.
