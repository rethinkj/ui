- With `TabGroups.splits(true)`, a tab dragged over a group's content in its own window shows drop zones (`#zone value=left|right|top|bottom|center`): the outer third toward an edge splits the group there, the middle docks the tab at the group's end, also over content with its own `onDrop`. A tab tears off into a new window only once the pointer is outside every window of the app (`Tabs.Options.tearOffOutside`, which reads the new `Drag.outsideAll`; `Windows.Place` gains the same component), and Escape after tearing off the only tab of a split group restores the group, the split and its divider.
