- With `TabGroups.splits(true)`, dragging a tab over any group's panel in the drag's starting window now shows edge and center drop zones (`#zone` with `value=left|right|top|bottom|center`) above the tab's content to split to a side or dock at the group's end, tears off into a new window only once the pointer leaves every window of the app (`Tabs.Options.tearOffOutside`), and defers pruning an emptied source group until the drag commits so Escape restores the original group, split, and divider position.
