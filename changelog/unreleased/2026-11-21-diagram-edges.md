- Added: `Canvas.canvas` takes edges between ports of its nodes (`Canvas.Edge`, `Canvas.Port`). They are routed at right angles with rounded bends around the nodes in the way, each in a lane of its own, a self-reference as a loop on the free side. They end in crow's foot markers or an arrow (`Canvas.Marker`), stand out or step back (`Canvas.Emphasis`), and show the app's text in a tooltip on hover; their sizes are in `EdgeStyle`.
