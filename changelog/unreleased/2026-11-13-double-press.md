- Added `El.onDoublePress(handler)`, which runs when the second press of a double press counts on an element, after its `onPress` handler. `El.revealRow(index)` now also works on a scroll area that is not a list of rows, where it keeps the child with that index in view whenever the index changes. A wheel that turns only up and down scrolls an area that scrolls only sideways (`overflow-x: scroll` without `overflow-y: scroll`), such as a row of tabs, sideways.
