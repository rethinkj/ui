- The first `GpuPresenter` of a thread now takes the GPU device and makes the window's surface when it draws its first frame, not when it is made, so that the app builds that frame while the device is being finished; on Windows the first frame comes about 70 ms sooner. What can fail there, such as wgpu-native that cannot be loaded or no adapter that can present to the window, is now thrown by that first `present`, inside `WindowLoop.run`, instead of by the constructor. A presenter for a window that opens next to others still takes the device when it is made.
