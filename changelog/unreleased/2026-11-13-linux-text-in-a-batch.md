- On X11 and Wayland, keys typed right after the click, Tab or key that takes the focus into a text field, or opens a dialog whose field takes it, reach the field with their text, also when they arrive in one batch with it (a fast typist, a password manager, `xdotool`). The window turns text input on as SDL queues a press or key and the loop turns it off again after the frame unless a field has the focus; the text of a key that went elsewhere, as Space that pressed a button, is dropped. Windows did this already.
