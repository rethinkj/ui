- On Windows, the first frame of an app appears about 0.4 s sooner: `rethinkj-ui-natives-windows-x64` now carries the DirectX Shader Compiler 1.9.2609 (`dxcompiler.dll`), which compiles the render pipeline in about 130 ms instead of the nearly 600 ms of FXC, the compiler built into Windows. The JAR grows by about 10 MB. DXC comes under the LLVM Release License (University of Illinois/NCSA), whose text is in the JAR under `META-INF/rethinkj-ui/natives/windows-x64/licenses/`. Libraries loaded with `-Drethinkj.ui.natives.dir=<folder>` whose `manifest.txt` does not list `dxcompiler` keep compiling with FXC.
