- **Breaking:** `Theme`'s default look is layered: the window's background is a page under cards, quiet bands inside a card, and raised controls and popups. `Theme.Surfaces` has a `band`, `Theme.Borders` is `subtle`, `strong` and `control` (the edge of buttons and popups) without `focus` and `selected`, `Theme.Accent` has an `edge` (the focus ring, the active tab's line, a selected card's edge), `Theme.Elevation` has a `card` level, `Theme.Sizes` an `indicator`, and `Theme.Type` lost its three-argument constructor and `Surfaces` its five-argument one. Radii are 4, 6 and 10, grid rows 24 high, headings Inter SemiBold and strong text, column titles and buttons Inter Medium. `Theme.over` mixes in linear light, as the renderer blends, so an overlay gives one colour however a component draws it.
