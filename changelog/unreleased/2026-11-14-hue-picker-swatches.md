- New `Radio.swatches(theme, options, selected, onSelect)`, a radio group whose choices show an element each, such as a colour or an icon (`Radio.option(name, theme -> element)`), in a row that wraps, with the name in a tooltip; it has the roles, states and keys of `Radio.radioGroup`, a typed form and a form with a `Prop`. The chosen option has a round ring in the accent's edge colour, and the keyboard's focus is a rounded square around the option the arrows go on from. On it, `HuePicker.huePicker(theme, selected, [none,] onSelect)` chooses one of the theme's `Theme.Hue`s from round swatches, with a choice of no colour first when the app names one; `HuePicker.swatch(theme, hue)` is the swatch alone.
