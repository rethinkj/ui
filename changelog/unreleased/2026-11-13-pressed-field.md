- Changed `El.whenPressed` on a text field: the look now applies while a press selects in the field, from the pointer down to the pointer up, as CSS's `:active` does, and `App.pressed()` returns the field meanwhile; before, a field never showed it. Elements around the field still do not count such a press as theirs. The kit's text field has no pressed look; the focus look a press gives it is its feedback.
