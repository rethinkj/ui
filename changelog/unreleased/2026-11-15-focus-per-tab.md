- The focus is remembered per tab of `TabGroups`: focusing the panel or switching tabs goes back to where the focus was in that tab, or to its `defaultFocus()` element if it has not had the focus, instead of to an element of the same key in another tab. Each tab's content is now inside `#content-<n>`. When the focus leaves the tree, it goes to where it was last in the nearest container around it, as `app.focus(container)` does, and a `defaultFocus()` element that appears after `app.focus(container)` found none takes the focus unless it has moved.
