- Scripts can aim a pointer step at a point near an element's edge instead of its center: after a selector, `at` names an edge and a distance in logical pixels, as in `move role=treeitem label="audit" at top+2`, `up #row-3 at bottom-2` or `press #header-2 at right-4,bottom-2`. It works for every target, so `press`, `down`, `up`, `move`, `wheel`, `drop`, `focus` and `scroll` take it.
