- Resizing a window on X11 no longer shows other windows' content or a layout of the size before: the window answers the window manager's `_NET_WM_SYNC_REQUEST`, so that during a resize it shows each step only once the app has drawn it, and what a resize uncovers before that is the colour of the app's root instead of whatever lay there. `WindowLoop` does this by itself; a loop of one's own calls the new `Window.drawn()` after each frame, else the window manager waits a second for its answer at each step.
