- The kit has badges (`Badge`, with the token group `Theme.Badges`), popovers (`Popover`), a breadcrumb (`Breadcrumb`) and a toolbar (`Toolbar`) that move what does not fit into a menu, toggle and split buttons (`Button.toggle`, `Button.split`) and lists of properties (`Properties`); a single-line text field with `El.ellipsis()` is shortened while it does not have the focus.
